# Radar
<!-- part: Research and playbooks | order: 50 | slug: radar | route: /radar | tags: radar, research, niche profiles, dimension library, proven, emerging, radar briefs, ideas, idea score, hook, keyword, evidence, run log, winner radar, make this, pipeline -->

Radar is where you read the research HQ has collected: niche profiles, a library of findings marked proven or emerging, scored content ideas for each brand, and a log of research runs. Use it to choose what to make next, and tap "Make this" on a brief to turn it straight into a pipeline, HQ's step-by-step production job.

## Where to find it
- On a computer, tap "Radar" under "Growth & Marketing" in the left menu.
- On a phone, tap "More" in the bottom bar, then "Radar" under "Growth & Marketing".
- Radar shows every brand at once. The project picker at the top of the screen does not filter it.

## What you see
The page has four sections, from top to bottom.

- "Niche Profiles": a card for each profile with the brand's name, an "Updated" date, the title, a summary and a "Full research doc" link.
- "Dimension Library": a card for each finding, labeled "PROVEN", "EMERGING" or "UNCLASSIFIED", with a title, a summary and an "Evidence" link.
- "Radar Briefs": content ideas grouped under each brand's name, with brands in A to Z order and the highest "Idea score" first. Each card shows the score, the title, a small stage label such as idea, the "Hook", the "Primary keyword" and an "Evidence" list. A brief that already has a pipeline shows a "Pipeline" label; the others show a "Make this" button.
- "Run Log": a table of research runs with the columns "Brand", "Completed", "Items fetched" and "Status". On a phone, swipe the table sideways to see every column.

Long summaries are cut to four lines; "Expand summary" shows the rest and "Show less" folds it back. A section with nothing in it says "No records yet.", and a section that fails to load says "Research could not be loaded. Please try again later."

## How to use it
### Read the research
1. Scroll to "Niche Profiles", "Dimension Library" or "Radar Briefs".
2. Tap "Expand summary" to read a whole summary.
3. Tap "Full research doc" or "Evidence" to open the source in a new tab. On a brief, evidence notes that came from a YouTube video open that video.

### Turn a brief into a pipeline
1. Find the brief under "Radar Briefs" and tap "Make this". The "Create pipeline" form opens with the brief's title filled in and the brand's core niches ticked.
2. Check "Product type". HQ picks one from the brief's format: long-form video, short video, printable, book or article, and a social piece for anything else.
3. Check the "Destinations", "Monetization" and "Promotion" chips, the "Level" and the "Money destination". The "Starting a pipeline" article explains each field.
4. Tap "Create pipeline". You see "Pipeline created" and HQ opens the new pipeline's page. Back on Radar, the brief now shows "Pipeline" instead of "Make this".

### Check that research is coming in
1. Scroll down to "Run Log".
2. Read the newest rows: when each run finished, how many items it fetched, and its status.

## Words on this screen
- Niche profile: a research write-up about one niche or audience, tied to a brand.
- Dimension: one finding in the research library.
- PROVEN, EMERGING, UNCLASSIFIED: the label on a dimension. HQ reads it from the dimension's title: PROVEN or EMERGING when that word is in the title, UNCLASSIFIED when neither is.
- Radar brief: a scored idea for one piece of content for one brand.
- Idea score: the brief's score from 0 to 100. Higher is stronger.
- Hook: the opening line or angle meant to grab attention.
- Primary keyword: the main search phrase the piece aims at.
- Evidence: the notes and links that back up a finding or a brief.
- Pipeline: a production job in HQ, made of ordered steps for you and for Claude that are built from playbooks.
- Items fetched: how many items a research run pulled in.

## Good to know
- Radar only reads research; it never edits it. The one thing it creates is a pipeline, when you use "Make this".
- Each Radar brief is also a content item, so it also appears on that brand's Content screen, and under "Briefs" on the brand's "Intel" tab while it is still at the idea stage.
- A playbook that is still Planned becomes a step that reads "Method not written yet" followed by its name, so check the "(planned)" chips before you create.
- "Run Log" lists Winner Radar research runs. The monthly Radar run adds one entry per brand it researched, on the 1st of each month.
