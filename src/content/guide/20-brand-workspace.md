# The brand workspace
<!-- part: Running a brand | order: 20 | slug: brand-workspace | route: /project/:venueId | tags: brand, brand page, brand workspace, open a brand, tabs, tab row, header, ultimate goal, active, parked, ownership, setup wizard, log an idea, content mode -->

The brand workspace is the home page for one of your own brands. It gathers everything about that brand behind one row of tabs, from its numbers and accounts to its products, content, research, playbooks and files. Open it whenever you want to work on a single brand instead of looking across all of them.

## Where to find it

- Open "Portfolio" (bottom bar on a phone, side menu on a computer), make sure "My brands" is selected, and tap the brand's card.
- The address is /project/ followed by the brand's code. Every tab except "Overview" adds ?tab= and the tab's name (for example ?tab=setup), so a bookmark can open a brand straight on one tab.
- The "Back to" link on a pipeline page and the "Open the Setup tab" link in a traffic check notice also lead here.

Only brand projects get the tabs. A brand project is one whose type is internal brand, content channel, app build or service offer. A client project opens at the same kind of address but shows the older client setup page instead.

## What you see

The brand header sits at the top:
- The brand's name, with a small badge showing its type, such as "INTERNAL BRAND" or "CONTENT CHANNEL".
- "Active" and "Parked" buttons, and a dropdown showing "Owned", "Client", "Family" or "Partner". Only admins see these. Everyone else sees a "Parked" badge when the brand is parked.
- A "Content:" badge with the content mode saved in the brand's kit (growth, baseline or parked), when one is set.
- Two links: "Log an idea" opens the page for logging a new idea, and "Brand kit" opens the Brand Kit page for this brand.
- "Ultimate goal": one line saying what winning looks like for this brand.
- A small line under the goal with the brand's money model, when one is recorded. It cannot be changed here.
- A live badge. It reads "LIVE — capturing leads" when the brand's go-live steps are finished, or "Go-live" with a count such as 3/5 when they are not. Next to it is the percent "fully configured" and a thin progress bar.
- A "Setup wizard" button on the right. It opens the step-by-step setup wizard for the go-live steps, such as lead capture.

On the Supreme Team Media brand only, a "Website, last 7 days" card follows the header, with "Page views", "CTA clicks", "Call requests", "First-response rate", "Inquiries by industry" and orders from the last 30 days.

Below the header is the tab row. On a phone it scrolls sideways, so swipe it left to reach the later tabs.
- "Overview": the brand at a glance, with headline numbers, what is waiting on you, the brand's Next 10, its scores and recent activity.
- "Setup": the map of every account and asset this brand should have, what is in place, and what is still to set up.
- "Channels": every social account, site and store on file, with follower numbers, posting targets and STALE warnings.
- "Avenues": the brand's money avenues (the ways it can earn), which ones are on, and what each is worth per hour of your time.
- "Products": the products linked to this brand, their listings, and a way to start making them.
- "Content": every content piece for this brand, with approvals.
- "Intel": research, meaning the niche profile, the audience's top pains and scored idea briefs.
- "Playbooks": the written playbooks this brand relies on and how complete they are.
- "Assets": brand kit files and links, brand documents and shared plans.

## How to use it

### To switch tabs
1. Tap a tab name in the tab row. On a phone, swipe the row left to see the rest.
2. The address changes with the tab, so the browser's back button and bookmarks keep your place.

### To set or change the ultimate goal
1. Tap the goal line, or "Set the ultimate goal" if none is set yet.
2. Type the goal in the box.
3. Tap "Save" or press Enter. HQ shows "Ultimate goal saved". Tap "Cancel" or press Escape to leave it as it was.

### To park a brand or make it active again
1. Tap "Parked" in the header. HQ shows "Parked for now".
2. To bring the brand back, tap "Active". HQ shows "Marked active".

### To change who owns a brand
1. Open the dropdown next to the "Active" and "Parked" buttons.
2. Choose "Owned", "Client", "Family" or "Partner". HQ shows "Saved".

## Words on this screen

- Brand project: a project for one of your own brands, as opposed to a client project.
- "Active" and "Parked": whether you are focusing on the brand right now. Parked brands are set aside, not deleted.
- "Owned", "Client", "Family", "Partner": whose brand it is.
- "Content:": the brand's content mode from its kit. The Brand Kit page describes the modes this way: Growth gets full weekly production, Baseline runs evergreen work on the clock, Parked gets none.
- "Ultimate goal": the one-line statement of what winning looks like for the brand.
- "Go-live": the required setup steps for capturing leads. The count shows how many are done.
- "fully configured": how much of the optional, fuller setup is done, as a percent.

## Good to know

- Opening a brand makes it the selected project across HQ, so pages such as the Brand Kit page then show this brand.
- Parking a brand takes its steps out of "Next 10" and "Waiting on me" on the Today page, and its card moves into the "Parked" fold on Portfolio. Parking does not change the brand's content mode, and the brand still shows on the Production Board.
- Not confirmed: which screens use the ownership choice. None of the brand's tabs change when you change it.
- If the brand cannot be loaded, the page says "Project not found" and offers a "Back to Portfolio" button.
