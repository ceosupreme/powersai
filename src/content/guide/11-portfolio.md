# Portfolio

Portfolio is your directory and the place to see every brand and client side by side. Use it to spot which brand needs you, which is earning and which still has setup gaps, then tap a card to open that brand. The "Clients" tab keeps the older client scorecards.

## Where to find it

- HQ opens here after you sign in. Side menu: "Workspace", then "Portfolio". On a phone, "Portfolio" is in the bottom bar. The address is /portfolio, and the "Supreme Team Media" name at the top of the side menu also leads here.
- The project picker is hidden on this screen because Portfolio always shows everything. The week picker stays.

## What you see

From the top:

- A "Welcome to HQ" card with "Take the tour", "What needs me today" (opens Dashboard) and "Launch checklist", plus a line such as "26 projects in HQ · Tour not started · Checklist 0 items done". It goes away once you dismiss it with the X, or once you have finished the tour and ticked at least one checklist item.
- The heading "Portfolio Overview" with "Week of" and the week chosen in the header.
- Three tabs: "My brands" (open by default), "Clients" and "Prospects".
- A summary strip: "Revenue", "Needs Attention" and "Lowest Project", plus "Data Confidence" on the Clients tab.
- A closed "Portfolio Snapshot" panel with weekly sales, ticket, tip, order, guest, turn time, void and labor totals.

On "My brands" you then see type chips ("All", "Brands", "Channels", "Apps", "Services", each with a count), "Sort" with a menu, the "All brands: waiting on you" box (up to eight open steps across your active brands, most urgent first, each with its brand name and a tag "P1" to "P4" or "No priority"), one card per active brand, a "Parked" section (open by default) with parked brands outlined and tagged "Parked", and "Revenue this month by project", listing each brand with revenue recorded this month, highest first.

Each brand card shows, top to bottom:

- The brand name, then the overall score with its letter grade and the change since the week before, such as "↑4 WoW".
- Data lines: how many KPIs (or pillars) were measured and for which week, and "As of" a date, with "stale" added when the data is eight or more days old.
- "3 live channels · 12.4K followers", or "No channels on file".
- The Setup line, such as "Setup: 7 of 12 in place, Caution".
- Money earned "this month".
- One small box per pillar with its grade (or "--"), labeled with the first letters of the pillar's name, such as "BP" for Brand Presence or "S" for Setup.
- "Potential" as a percent with a bar.
- A footer: "Waiting on you" (large number), "Channels live" ("4 of 6", plus "· 1 stale" in red), "Products listed" ("2 of 5"), "Published this month", "Revenue this month" and "Next move".

On "Clients" you see "Project Scorecards" as cards or a table, then a "GM Performance" table and a "Revenue by Project" chart comparing this week with the week before. A client card shows the name, a colored status dot, the score and grade, the week's sales with the change from the same week last year, four grade boxes "R", "L", "O" and "G", and "View →".

## How to use it

### To switch between brands, clients and prospects

1. Tap "My brands", "Clients" or "Prospects" under the heading.

### To show one type of brand

1. On "My brands", tap "Brands", "Channels", "Apps" or "Services". The number on each chip is how many brands of that type you have.
2. Tap "All" to show every type again.

### To sort your brands

1. On "My brands", tap the menu next to "Sort".
2. Choose one of the eight orders below. Parked brands are sorted the same way inside their own section, and HQ remembers your choice on this device.

The eight orders:

- "Waiting on you": most waiting first; ties go to the higher score. This is the default.
- "Score": highest overall score first.
- "Money this month": most revenue this month first.
- "Potential": highest Potential first.
- "Live channels": most channels marked live first.
- "Followers": most followers, all channels added together, first.
- "Setup gaps": most items still to set up first.
- "Name (A to Z)": alphabetical.

For every order except "Waiting on you", brands with no value go last and ties are broken by name.

### To open a brand

1. Tap anywhere on its card.
2. The brand workspace opens, and the brand becomes the selected project in the header.

### To act on what is waiting

1. In "All brands: waiting on you", tap a step.
2. A step that belongs to a pipeline opens that pipeline's page; any other step opens the brand workspace.

### To view clients as cards or a table

1. Open the "Clients" tab.
2. Tap "Cards" or "Table" beside "Project Scorecards". In the table, tap a column heading to sort by it and tap it again to flip the order.

### To open a client and get back

1. Tap a client card, a table row, a row under "GM Performance", or "Lowest Project" when it shows a red pillar grade.
2. The client's project page opens in the client layout. To return, tap "← Portfolio" at the left of the header.

## Words on this screen

- Score: the weighted average of the brand's pillar scores. Pillars with no score are skipped, not counted as zero.
- Grade: A for 90 and up, B for 80 to 89, C for 70 to 79, D for 60 to 69, F below 60.
- WoW: week over week, the change from the week before.
- Pillar: one scored area of a brand. The Setup pillar is filled automatically every night from the brand's setup map and counts for 20 percent of the score.
- Setup line: how many of the accounts and assets the brand needs are in place, then a gate word (explained under Good to know). If no gate word comes back, the line ends with how many are left "to set up" instead. Brands that need nothing show no Setup line.
- "Potential": the share of the brand's money lanes that are switched on, weighted by importance; a half-done lane counts half.
- "Waiting on you" (card): open steps for this brand that are not Claude's, approved or not, plus content pieces ready for your approval.
- "stale": a channel with a posting goal whose last post is older than that goal allows, or that has never posted.
- "Products listed": linked products that are listed somewhere, out of all products linked to this brand.
- "Published this month": posts confirmed as published this calendar month.
- "Next move": the top step in this brand's Next 10.
- Parked: a brand you have set aside. It stays on Portfolio but drops out of the waiting box and Dashboard's lists.
- Status dot (client card): red and pulsing for a critical insight this week, orange for high, gold for medium, green for none.
- "No POS": a client whose data confidence is under 40 percent, so its score is hidden.
- R, L, O, G: the four areas of the client weekly scorecard, Revenue, Labor, Operations and Guest. The Insights filter calls the last two "Delivery" and "Client Experience".

## Good to know

- Brand cards use each brand's last completed week that has data, not the week in the header. The data lines show the real week; the week in the section heading comes from the header.
- The Setup line ends with one of three words: "Ready to grow" for a setup score of 70 or more, "Caution" for 50 to 69, and "Set up first" for under 50. See "How scores work".
- The card's "Waiting on you" count, the "All brands: waiting on you" box (steps only, no content) and Dashboard's "Waiting on me" (decisions only) count slightly different things, so their numbers can differ.
- The type chips also filter the waiting box, but not "Revenue this month by project". Chip counts include parked brands.
- The money "this month" near the top of a card and "Revenue this month" in its footer are the same figure.
- On a phone, pillar boxes show only initials; on a computer, hover a box to see the pillar's full name.
- The summary strip and "Portfolio Snapshot" come from the client scorecards and sales data of every project, whatever tab is open. Your own brands have no such data, so these usually read "—", "Awaiting data", "0 ✓" and "No scored projects".
- The "Revenue" tile says "vs last week", but the percent it shows is the average change against the same week last year.
- The three green dots under "Data Confidence" ("Toast POS", "7shifts", "Asana") are fixed labels, not live connection checks.
- In "GM Performance", "Avg Score" is just this week's score and "Trend" always shows "--".
- The "Revenue by Project" chart includes every project, not only clients.
- The "Prospects" tab always shows a note with an "Open Prospect Dock" link, because prospect projects are not loaded on this screen.
- If you open a client and come back through the menu instead of "← Portfolio", Portfolio shows that client's detail page ("Weekly Scorecard", "Signal Breakdown", "Recent Insights", "Pending Actions"). Tap the back arrow beside the client's name to see the full Portfolio.
- The tab, the type chip and the cards or table choice reset each visit; only the sort is remembered.

## Distinguishing brands
Brand cards have consistent colored headers with large initials and their full names. Color stays with the brand when its name changes. Portfolio is the directory; each brand already has its own project workspace. Client businesses also have projects.
