# Starting a pipeline
<!-- part: Making things | order: 41 | slug: create-pipeline | route: none | tags: create pipeline, start a pipeline, new job, make this, start production, turn this on, set up, set it up, top 3, product type, destinations, monetization, promotion, niche, level, money destination, traffic check -->

Every pipeline in HQ starts from a button next to the thing you want to make or switch on, and most of those buttons open the same "Create pipeline" form. The form chooses the playbooks the steps are built from, then creates the job and opens its page. Use this article to know which button to press and what each field in the form does.

## Where to find it

The buttons that start a pipeline:
- "Make this" on a brief: on a brand's "Intel" tab under "Briefs", and on the Radar page under "Radar Briefs".
- "Make this" on a pain: on a brand's "Intel" tab under "Top pains", and on the Answer Bank page.
- "Start production" on a product: on a brand's "Products" tab.
- "Turn this on" on a money avenue: on a brand's "Avenues" tab.
- "Set up" in a channel's drawer: on a brand's "Channels" tab.
- "Set it up" and "Set up the top 3" on a brand's "Setup" tab. These skip the form and start the job at once.

Once a job exists, "Make this" on a brief and "Start production" on a product turn into "Pipeline", which opens the job instead of making a new one.

## What you see

The "Create pipeline" form has:
- A line under the title. Content and product jobs say "Pick the playbooks. Steps are built from them in order." Avenue and channel setup jobs say "This kind of job uses a fixed five-step plan."
- "Title": filled in from where you started. You can change it. If you clear it, HQ uses the original title.
- "Niche": chips for the niche playbooks tagged for this brand. The brand's core niches start ticked. "None on the shelf." means no niche is tagged for the brand.
- "Product type": a list of format playbooks, such as a printable or a short video. Content and product jobs only. It is required.
- "Destinations": chips for platform playbooks, such as Etsy or YouTube. Content and product jobs only.
- "Monetization": chips for money path playbooks. Content and product jobs only.
- "Promotion": chips for promotion playbooks. Content and product jobs only.
- A traffic check notice under the chips when it applies, starting "Before this drives traffic:", with an "Open the Setup tab" link.
- "Level": "Level 1", "Level 2" or "Level 3". It starts on Level 2.
- "Money destination": free text for where the money lands. The box shows "e.g. Etsy shop" as an example.
- "Cancel" and "Create pipeline". For content and product jobs, "Create pipeline" stays grey until a product type is chosen.

Playbooks that are planned but not written yet show "(planned)" after their name. A ticked chip shows a check mark and fills in.

## How to use it

### To start a pipeline from a brief
1. Open the brand's "Intel" tab, or the Radar page.
2. Tap "Make this" on the brief.
3. Check the form (see the last task below) and tap "Create pipeline".

### To start a pipeline from a pain
1. Open the brand's "Intel" tab, or the Answer Bank page.
2. Tap "Make this" on the pain. HQ creates a new idea called "Answer:" followed by the pain, then opens the form with it as a social piece.
3. Check the form and tap "Create pipeline".

### To start production on a product
1. Open the brand's "Products" tab.
2. Tap "Start production" on the product.
3. Check the form and tap "Create pipeline".

### To switch on a money avenue
1. Open the brand's "Avenues" tab.
2. Tap "Turn this on" on the avenue.
3. Check the title, niche, level and money destination, then tap "Create pipeline".

### To set up one channel
1. Open the brand's "Channels" tab and tap the channel's row.
2. Tap "Set up" in the drawer.
3. Check the title, niche, level and money destination, then tap "Create pipeline".

### To start setup from the Setup tab
1. Open the brand's "Setup" tab.
2. Tap "Set it up" on an item, or "Set up the top 3" and then "Start all 3".
3. HQ creates the setup job straight away. Tap "Open pipeline" in the message to see it.

### To fill in the Create pipeline form
1. Check the "Title".
2. Tick or untick niches under "Niche".
3. Choose a "Product type" if the form asks for one.
4. Tick or untick chips under "Destinations", "Monetization" and "Promotion".
5. Read any traffic check notice. Tap "Open the Setup tab" if you want to fix the gap first.
6. Pick a "Level" and type a "Money destination" if you know it.
7. Tap "Create pipeline". HQ shows "Pipeline created" and opens the new pipeline page.

## Words on this screen

- Pipeline: a production job, the step-by-step plan for making one thing for one brand.
- Playbook: a written method in HQ's library. Each chip in the form is a playbook.
- Niche: a playbook about an audience. Niches give the job context and go into its recipe, but add no steps.
- "Product type": the format playbook. Its steps form the core of the job.
- "Destinations": where the piece or product will live, such as a platform or store. Their steps are added after the product type's.
- "Monetization": how the job earns, such as direct sale, lead magnet or affiliate. These playbooks add extra steps near the end.
- "Promotion": how the job gets attention, such as a video that points to the product. These add steps at the very end.
- Traffic check: a warning that a chosen money or promotion playbook needs an account the brand has not set up. It reads "Before this drives traffic:", then the playbook's name, "needs" and the account (or "one of:" and a list), and ends "which isn't set up for this brand yet."
- "Level": the production level for the job, 1 to 3.
- "Money destination": where the money from this job is meant to land.
- Fixed five-step plan: the set steps used for avenue and channel setup jobs, whatever playbooks exist.

## Good to know

- The form fills itself in from where you started. Products start with their listing outlets as destinations, direct sale for monetization and video to product for promotion. Briefs and pains start with lead magnet and affiliate for monetization and post to lead magnet for promotion. When nothing else is given, destinations start from the brand's channels that are live, dormant or planned. A default only stays ticked if that playbook is on the shelf.
- The steps come out in this order: "Brand rules check" (Claude), the product type's steps, each destination's steps, then the steps added by monetization and by promotion.
- The traffic check only warns; it never stops you. An account marked "Exists, needs access" does not count as set up for the check.
- "Make this" on a pain creates the idea the moment you tap it. If you cancel the form, the idea stays on the brand's list.
- Repeating "Turn this on" or "Set up" reuses the active pipeline for that target. New jobs, their steps and any new Answer Bank content item are saved together.
- Jobs started from the "Setup" tab are created at Level 1, with no niches, because they skip the form.
- The job retains the recipe and playbook versions. Creation fails clearly if a required playbook is missing or a Written method has no usable steps.
- Creating a pipeline never publishes or posts anything.

Opening "Make this" only prepares the form. Canceling it creates no content item. A new item is created only when the pipeline is successfully saved. Planned methods are clearly labeled and retain their explicit writing prerequisite.
