# Where everything lives
<!-- part: Reference | order: 97 | slug: where-things-live | route: none | tags: drive, folders, files, email, life pulse, metricool, stripe, vidiq, lovable, where is, find -->

HQ keeps its records in the app, its files in Google Drive, and its reports in your email and Life Pulse. This article is the map: where to look for any record, file or report, and which outside services HQ is connected to.

## In HQ

- Every brand: open "Portfolio" and tap the brand's card. The brand workspace has the tabs "Overview", "Setup", "Channels", "Avenues", "Products", "Content", "Intel", "Playbooks" and "Assets".
- Accounts, handles and follower numbers: the brand's "Channels" tab.
- What a brand still needs to set up: the brand's "Setup" tab.
- Pipelines and their steps: the brand workspace, the pipeline page each job opens, and "Production Board".
- Methods for every format, platform, money path and niche, plus winning Plays: "Playbooks".
- Ideas and research briefs: "Radar", and each brand's "Intel" tab.
- Audience questions and buyer complaints: "Answer Bank".
- Money: "Channel Revenue", "Products", "Offers" and "Affiliate Programs".
- Colors, fonts, logos and voice: "Brand Kit".
- Clients and leads: "CRM", "Capture Inbox" and "Automation Inbox".
- Your to-do list across everything: "Dashboard".

## In Google Drive

- "Supreme Team HQ - Brands": one folder for each brand, holding every file the production crew makes for it. Files are named with the job, the step and the date, so the newest version is easy to spot.
- "Supreme Team HQ - Playbooks": a readable Google Doc for every written playbook, named with the shelf, the title and the version.
- "Supreme Team HQ - Playbook Inbox": drop your own material here for the playbooks. The "Merged" folder inside it holds everything that has already been used.
- "Supreme Team HQ - Plans and Specs": build plans and specs. It is also the place to keep the PDF copy of this guide.

Every file the crew makes is also linked on the pipeline step that made it, so you rarely need to dig through Drive.

## In your email

- "Production run: what got made": after a night when the production crew did anything. No email means nothing happened.
- "Monthly Radar: this month's picks across your brands": on the 1st of each month, with the top briefs for each researched brand.

## In Life Pulse

Life Pulse is your private dashboard, separate from HQ. HQ's crews add a card there when something needs you, with a button that opens the right place: "Open pipeline" for a step that is now yours, "Open playbook" for a decision about a playbook, and "Open HQ" for the monthly Radar picks.

## Outside services connected to HQ

- Metricool: pulls follower and post numbers for the accounts connected to it. Not every account can be connected on the current plan, so the rest get their numbers from screenshots on the "Channels" tab or by hand.
- Stripe: online checkouts and payments. HQ double-checks completed checkouts every hour.
- vidIQ: YouTube research for the monthly Radar run, inside a fixed credit cap.
- Google Drive and Gmail: where the crews save files and send reports.
- Lovable: the builder and database behind HQ. Bigger screen changes are built there, which costs credits, so Claude always asks you first.
- GitHub: a copy of HQ's code. Small fixes are made there directly at no cost and go live after a publish.

## Scheduled tasks

The crews that run on a schedule live in your Claude account, not in HQ. The article "The crews that run while you sleep" lists every one with its time and limits. Ask Claude in any chat to pause, resume or change one.

## This guide

- In HQ: "Guide" in the menu under "System".
- As a PDF: Claude sends a fresh copy in the chat whenever the guide is rebuilt. Keep it in "Supreme Team HQ - Plans and Specs".
- The older, shorter help articles are still available from the link at the bottom of every Guide article, in the "Help Center".

## Good to know

- Passwords for your platforms and accounts are never kept in HQ, in Drive or in chat. You log in directly on each platform.
- When you cannot find a file, open the pipeline step that should have made it. The link on the step always points at the current file.
