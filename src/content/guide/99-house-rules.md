# House rules
<!-- part: Reference | order: 99 | slug: house-rules | route: none | tags: rules, credits, money, publishing, passwords, logins, facebook, done, approval, standards, assistant -->

These are the standing rules HQ, Claude, the crews and anyone helping you work by. They protect your money, your accounts and your time. Share this page with any assistant before they start working in HQ.

## Money and credits

- Nothing that spends money or paid credits happens without your go.
- Small screen fixes are made directly in HQ's code at no cost. Bigger builds go through Lovable, which costs credits, so Claude tells you what it wants to build and the rough cost first, then waits for your yes.
- vidIQ research credits are used only with your go. The monthly Radar run has a standing cap of 90 vidIQ credits per run that you approved. The crews never use vidIQ's generation tools.
- No paid image, video or voice tools are used by the crews.

## Publishing

- Nothing is published, scheduled, listed or sent to the public without your go.
- The one standing exception is the Coastal Beauties blog crew, which you set up to publish one article on Mondays and Thursdays on its own.
- A piece of content is approved only by you. The crews never approve their own work.

## Logins and passwords

- Never paste a password, key or login code into a chat or into HQ. Log in directly on the platform.
- Claude, the crews and HQ will never ask you for a password. If something ever does, do not answer it.
- Your personal Facebook profile is never automated. Only pages and brand accounts are worked on.

## What Done means

- A step is Done only when its output exists and is linked on the step.
- Planned is not done. A channel that is planned or needs access is still a gap until it is on file.
- Claude never marks one of your steps Done for you.

## Your material and your playbooks

- Your own notes and material outrank outside opinions and Claude's earlier text, except official platform rules and legal or safety requirements, which are kept with your note beside them.
- Nothing you add is ever deleted. Material that cannot be used is marked rejected with the reason.
- A written playbook is never overwritten. Every change is a new version, and the old one is kept.

## How Claude talks to you

- Reports and messages are plain text in complete sentences, with no bold or italics, written to read easily on a phone.
- Statuses are always words. A color is never the only signal.
- When you send a message, Claude answers it first before starting any work.
- When Claude needs a decision, it gives you simple tap-through choices with the recommended one first.
- Every change comes with step-by-step checks you can follow, each with a clear pass or fail.

## Contact and privacy

- The scheduled crews never contact anyone but you.
- Client proposal pages are not changed by any build unless you ask for it.

## For an assistant or a bot working in HQ

1. Read this page and the article "What HQ is and how it fits together".
2. For setup work, open the brand's "Setup" tab and use "Copy setup brief". The brief lists every gap with its playbook and the steps to follow.
3. Create or claim each account with the brand's own email and its usual handle, and add the bio, profile image and link from the brand kit.
4. Record each finished account on the "Setup" tab with "I already have it".
5. Never paste passwords into chat or HQ. Log in directly on the platform.
6. Ask the owner before anything that costs money, publishes, or cannot be undone.
