# Help Center and Launch Checklist
<!-- part: Tools and system | order: 88 | slug: help-and-launch | route: /launch | tags: help, help center, articles, launch, launch checklist, checklist, setup, go live, progress, automation bundle, tips, setup wizard, help and guidance -->

The Help Center is HQ's older set of short help articles, and the Launch Checklist is a list of 24 setup and go-live steps that you tick off as you finish them. Use the Help Center for quick background on a feature (this Guide is newer and more complete), and the Launch Checklist when setting HQ up or before giving anyone else a login. The help settings that control tips and the setup wizard are covered here too.

## Where to find it

- Launch Checklist on a computer: in the side menu, under "System", tap "Launch Checklist".
- Launch Checklist on a phone: tap "More" in the bottom bar, then "Launch Checklist" under "System".
- Help Center: it is no longer in the menus, because "Guide" took its place. Open Admin Panel, "Settings", "Help & Guidance", then tap "Help Center" under "Go to". You can also add /help to the end of the site address.
- Help settings: Admin Panel, "Settings", "Help & Guidance". On a phone it is the last sub-tab, the question-mark icon.

## What you see

### Help Center
- The heading "Help Center" and "Step-by-step guides for the features in this app."
- A search box ("Search: crm, brand, export, archive, capture, pillar, lead…").
- A list of 33 articles, each with a title and a one-line summary. The open article shows its title, summary, tag chips and sections. "How this OS fits together" is open when the screen loads.
- On a phone the list comes first and the open article sits below it.

### Launch Checklist
- The heading "Launch Checklist" and "Your real remaining steps before going live."
- A progress card such as "5 of 24 complete" with a percentage bar.
- 24 cards, each with a checkbox, a title, a short description and usually a link such as "Open Portfolio". The first 16 are setup steps (projects, types, pillars, the lead qualifier, Weekly Review, CRM, automations, Brand Kit, Growth Audit, revenue, content and reports). The last 8 are go-live checks (the domain, a Capture Inbox test, a security check, a full backup, the public pages, AI routing, archive protection and a Guide spot-check). Ticked cards fade and are crossed out.

### Help & Guidance
- "Show help across the app", a switch for the tips and info boxes.
- "Dismissed help items", the number of tips you closed, with "Reset all".
- "Setup wizard", showing "Not run yet.", "Completed" or "Skipped" with a date, and "Re-launch wizard".
- A "Go to" card with "Guide", "Help Center" and "Launch Checklist".

## How to use it

### Look something up in the Help Center
1. Type a word in the search box. It matches titles, summaries, tags and article text, and shows "No articles match." when nothing fits.
2. Tap an article in the list.
3. On a phone, scroll down past the list to read it.

### Work through the Launch Checklist
1. Read a card and tap its link to go to the right screen.
2. Do the step there.
3. Come back and tick the card's checkbox. Untick it to undo.

### Apply an automation bundle from the checklist
1. Choose a single project in the project picker in the header. The bundle box only appears then.
2. On the card "Apply an Automation Bundle to the project", find "Apply a bundle".
3. Pick one under "Bundle". Bundles made for the project's type are marked "suggested".
4. Tap "Apply". If the project already has some of the same automations, choose "Keep existing" (the safe choice), "Replace with defaults" or "Cancel".
5. You see "Bundle applied" with how many were added, replaced and kept. Then tick the card yourself.

### Turn help tips on or off, or rerun the setup wizard
1. Open "Help & Guidance".
2. Turn "Show help across the app" off to hide every tip and info box. It also stops the setup wizard from appearing.
3. Tap "Reset all" to bring back tips you dismissed.
4. Tap "Re-launch wizard" to see the setup wizard again. It opens the next time you go to Portfolio; if you already closed it during this visit, reload the page first.

## Words on this screen

- Article: one Help Center page about a feature.
- Tags: short topic words on an article, used by search.
- Automation bundle: a ready-made set of automations, such as follow-ups, reactivation and review requests, applied to a project in one step.
- Suggested: a bundle made for the project's type.
- Keep existing: leaves automations the project already has as they are.
- Replace with defaults: overwrites those automations with the bundle's settings.
- Setup wizard: a short welcome tour of the setup steps. It opens on its own on Portfolio for a login that has not seen it yet.

## Good to know

- Checklist ticks are manual. Nothing ticks itself, even when the work is done.
- Your ticks are saved to your login and follow you to every device. Another person's ticks are separate.
- "Open Integrations" opens Admin Panel, "Settings", "Projects". There is no Integrations tab; each project's integrations are in its "Edit" form under "Integrations".
- "Open Qualifier Fields" opens the "Pillars" tab, where "Qualifier Fields" is one of the inner tabs.
- "Open public site" sends anyone signed in to Portfolio. Check the public pages in a private or signed-out browser window.
- The security card points to Sync Health, but Sync Health does not show security rules.
- Much of the Help Center text is older than the screens. For example, it still says Help sits in the side menu and that inbound leads can be deleted from a dialog, which the screen does not offer. When they disagree, trust the screen and this Guide.
- For other roles, the Launch Checklist page follows the "Project Home" switch in Permissions, while its menu link follows "Dashboard".
