# Proposal Pages
<!-- part: Clients and leads | order: 79 | slug: proposals | route: /proposal/:slug | tags: proposal, proposal page, unlisted link, video proposal, agreement request, approve the plan, print, pdf, proposal builder, proposal document -->

A proposal page is a private web page made for one prospect, with your walkthrough video at the top, then the plan, the investment and a form to request the agreement. The prospect opens it from a link you send; it is not listed on the site or shown to search engines. When the prospect requests the agreement, the request lands in your CRM like any other lead.

## Where to find it

- Each proposal lives at its own path, /proposal/ followed by a short name for that proposal.
- There is no menu item and no screen in HQ for making these pages. Each proposal is written into the website itself as its own page and then published, so ask Claude when you need a new one.
- A wrong or removed link shows the site's "page not found" screen.
- Separate from these pages, the "Proposals" cards in a CRM company's panel, on a client's project page and on the Leak Stack make a printable proposal document inside HQ (see the last task below).

## What you see

- A top bar with your company mark and "Proposal for" the prospect's company.
- The opening: who it was prepared for and when, the program name, the headline, a subtitle, the video, three headline facts and a short note under them.
- Jump links: "How it works", "Who does what", "Your investment", "The pilot" and "Get started".
- The sections: your opening note and the outcomes, the "How it works" steps, the program blocks, the monthly work and output, "Who does what" in two columns, the markets with an example calendar, the investment with an "Approve the plan" button, the pilot phases, and the "Getting started" list.
- Side actions: "Arrange a quick proposal review" and "Ask … a question" (both open the prospect's email app addressed to you), "Download the two-page PDF" when a PDF is attached, and "Print or save this page as a PDF".
- The "Request your agreement" form: "Your full name", "Title" (optional), "Company" (filled in already), "Best phone number" and "Email for the agreement and invoice". The fine print reads "We send the agreement for your review. Nothing is charged on this page."
- On a phone, a bar at the bottom offers "Your investment" and "Request the agreement".

## How to use it

### Make a new proposal page
1. Gather the content: the program name, headline and subtitle; the prospect's company, contact and location; the date; a video link (YouTube, Loom or Vimeo, or a video file); an optional PDF; three headline facts; the journey steps; your opening note; outcomes; program blocks; monthly output; the improvement loop; who does what; markets; pricing lines and plain rules; pilot phases; next steps; the button and confirmation wording; and a short internal label for your alert.
2. Ask your builder to add it as a new entry in the proposal content file with a short, unique name for the link, then publish the site.
3. Open the link yourself on a phone and on a computer before you send it.

### Share it
1. Send the link by text or email.
2. Anyone with the link can open it, so share it only with the prospect.

### Handle an agreement request
1. You receive the usual new-inquiry email alert.
2. Open "CRM", then "Inbound", then "New". The lead's message starts with "PLAN APPROVED, AGREEMENT REQUESTED" and lists the person, title, company, phone, the email for the agreement and invoice, the time, and the terms shown on the page. Its type badge reads "agreement_request".
3. Send the agreement and invoice yourself. The page never charges or sends anything.

### Make a printable proposal inside HQ
1. On a CRM company, a client project page or the Leak Stack, find "Proposals" and tap "Generate proposal".
2. In "New proposal", check "Prospect name (on document)" and "Operation footprint", edit the "Intro line", tick the "Engines to install", pick leaks from the latest Leak Stack run or add "Manual leaks", choose a "Package", adjust "Price display (editable)" and the "Next step", then tap "Save draft".
3. The preview opens. Tap "Print / PDF" to save or print it. Back in the list, the send icon marks it "sent", the undo icon returns it to "draft", and the bin icon deletes it.

## Words on this screen

- Unlisted: reachable only by someone who has the link.
- Agreement request: the prospect's approval of the plan, sent from the form.
- Headline facts: the three short facts shown large near the top.
- Pilot: the trial period laid out in the proposal.
- draft, sent: the two states of a proposal document made inside HQ.
- Engines: "Acquisition Engine", "Retention Engine", "Command Center" and "Delivery Engine", the parts of the system a proposal document can include.
- Operation footprint: the size of the prospect's business, from "Solo owner-operator" to "Multi-location".

## Good to know

- Page views and button taps are recorded as site events. No screen shows the views for one proposal yet.
- Some section headings, the pilot headline and the example market calendar (including its home city) are fixed in the page design rather than in each entry, so they suit the current proposal. A proposal for a different kind of client needs those changed too.
- The request form deliberately sends the prospect no automatic thank-you email; the page shows its own confirmation.
- A video link from any other service shows no video. For a video file, the page also looks for a cover image with the same name ending in "-poster.jpg".
- Proposal documents inside HQ are shown only to owner and admin accounts, and the "Generate proposal" button appears only for admins.
