# Playbooks tab
<!-- part: Running a brand | order: 28 | slug: playbooks-tab | route: /project/:venueId | tags: playbooks, methods, written, thin, planned, niches, platforms, money paths, formats, waiting on, core, angle, playbook job, coverage -->

The "Playbooks" tab shows which written playbooks this brand depends on and how complete each one is. A playbook is a written method in HQ's playbook library that explains how to do one thing, such as run a platform, earn through a money path, serve a niche or make a format. Use this tab to find the thin or missing playbooks that hold this brand back, and to follow any playbook being written for it.

## Where to find it

- Open the brand, then tap "Playbooks" in the tab row. On a phone, swipe the tab row left to reach it.
- The address ends in ?tab=playbooks.

## What you see

- A summary line at the top, for example "This brand relies on 12 playbooks: 7 written, 3 thin, 2 planned."
- "Waiting on": playbook jobs for this brand, meaning jobs that write or update a playbook. Each shows its title, its job status and step, such as "Running · Step 2", and a "Pipeline" button. With none, it says "No playbook is being written for this brand."
- "Niches": the niche playbooks tagged for this brand. Each has its status word and a "Core" or "Angle" badge, with the angle written under it when there is one.
- "Platforms": one line per channel, showing the platform and handle, the channel's status ("Live", "Dormant", "Empty", "Planned" or "Needs access"), and the platform's playbook with its status word, or "No playbook".
- "Money paths": the brand's money avenues that are on, partly on or not on yet, grouped under the money path playbook that covers them, or under "No playbook yet". Each avenue shows "On", "Partly on" or "Not on yet".
- "Formats": the format playbooks (product types) used by this brand's pipelines.
- Every playbook name is a link. Tapping one opens the Playbooks page with that playbook open.

## How to use it

### To find the weak playbooks for this brand
1. Read the summary line for the number of thin and planned playbooks.
2. Scan each block for "Thin" and "Planned" badges.
3. Tap a playbook's name to open it on the Playbooks page and see what it is missing.

### To follow a playbook being written
1. Under "Waiting on", tap "Pipeline" on the job. Its pipeline page opens.

### To check which platforms have no playbook
1. Look down the "Platforms" block for "No playbook".

## Words on this screen

- Playbook: a written method in HQ's library that tells you and Claude how to do one thing. Pipelines build their steps from playbooks.
- "Written": an active playbook that is not past its review date and has enough depth: at least 1,500 characters of full text, a linked skill or pack, or five or more steps. Rules and promotion playbooks are short by design, so they count as written when they carry their one-line rule.
- "Thin": an active playbook that is not deep enough yet, or is past its review date.
- "Planned": listed in the library but not written yet.
- "Core": the brand's main niche.
- "Angle": a niche the brand serves from one side, with the angle written under it.
- Money path: a playbook for a way of earning, such as direct sales or affiliate links.
- Format: a playbook for a type of product or piece, such as a printable or a short video.
- Playbook job: a production job that writes or updates a playbook.

## Good to know

- The summary counts each playbook once, even when it appears in more than one block.
- Superseded and archived playbooks are left out.
- Money avenues whose state is unknown are left out of "Money paths".
- "Formats" only fills in once the brand has pipelines. Until then it says "No product types in this brand's pipelines yet."
- A platform's playbook is the channel's governing method when one is set; otherwise HQ matches the platform to the playbook for that platform.
- Playbook jobs show here and on the Playbooks page only. They are left out of the brand's other job buttons and its "Activity" list.
- Nothing in the brand workspace starts a playbook job. The nightly production run creates them on its own: for a planned playbook your material is waiting on, and for a method a pipeline is stuck on. They show up here once created.
- When a pipeline is built from a planned playbook, that playbook becomes a single Claude step called "Method not written yet:" followed by its name. Pipelines created after the playbook is written get its real steps; pipelines created before keep the placeholder step.
