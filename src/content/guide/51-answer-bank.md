# Answer Bank
<!-- part: Research and playbooks | order: 51 | slug: answer-bank | route: /answer-bank | tags: answer bank, audience pains, questions, pain score, intensity, search presence, gap, pillar, sources, unanswered, answered, refresh due, product idea, make this, content ideas -->

The Answer Bank keeps your audience's pains and questions in their own words, one list per brand, ranked by how much they hurt. Use it to decide what to answer next, and tap "Make this" to turn a pain straight into a piece of content.

## Where to find it
- On a computer, tap "Answer Bank" under "Growth & Marketing" in the left menu.
- On a phone, tap "More" in the bottom bar, then "Answer Bank" under "Growth & Marketing".
- It is for admin accounts only. Anyone else sees "The Answer Bank is available to admins only."
- You pick the brand on this screen; the project picker at the top does not change it.
- A brand's "Intel" tab also lists its eight highest-scoring pains under "Top pains".

## What you see
- The heading "Answer Bank" with the line "Your audience's pains and questions, in their words, ranked by how much they hurt.", and an "Add" button.
- Three dropdowns: the brand (your own brands that are switched on, starting with the first in A to Z order), the status ("Any status", "Unanswered", "Answered", "Refresh due") and the pillar ("Any pillar" or a pillar this brand uses).
- The entries, highest pain score first. Each shows the pain, its score out of 25 (such as 18/25), its status and pillar, a "Product idea" label when flagged, how many sources, and a "Make this" button.
- Tap an entry to open it and see its intensity, search and gap numbers, why it hurts, when it was first and last seen, "Source links", "Answered by", notes, and an "Edit" button.
- With no entries yet, the screen says "Nothing here yet. Tap Add to record a pain or question your audience has, with links to where you saw it."

## How to use it
### Find the pains worth answering first
1. Pick the brand.
2. Set the status to "Unanswered", or to "Refresh due" for answers that need updating.
3. Pick a pillar if you want one topic only.
4. Work from the top of the list; it is sorted by pain score.

### Add a pain or question
1. Pick the brand, then tap "Add".
2. Type the pain in "Pain or question (their words)". It is the only required field.
3. Fill in what you know: "Pillar", "Source links (one per line)", "Source count", "Pain score (0–25)", "First seen", "Last seen", "Intensity (0–5)", "Search presence (0–5)", "Gap (0–5)" and "Status".
4. Under "Why it hurts", tap each reason that applies: money, safety, time, embarrassment or frustration.
5. Switch on "Could be a product" if it could become something to sell, add any "Notes", and tap "Save".

### Change an entry
1. Tap the entry to open it, then tap "Edit".
2. Change what you need and tap "Save".

### Turn a pain into content
1. Tap "Make this" on the entry.
2. HQ adds an idea to the brand's Content list, titled "Answer:" followed by the first 80 characters of the pain.
3. The "Create pipeline" form, which starts a production job, opens with the product type set to a social piece. Check the niches, playbooks, level and money destination; the "Starting a pipeline" article explains each field.
4. Tap "Create pipeline". HQ opens the new pipeline's page.

## Words on this screen
- Pain: a problem, worry or question your audience has, in their words.
- Pillar: the content topic the pain belongs to.
- Pain score: how much it hurts, from 0 to 25. You type it in; HQ does not work it out from the other numbers.
- Intensity: how strongly people feel it, from 0 to 5.
- Search presence: how much people search for it, from 0 to 5.
- Gap: how poorly it is already answered elsewhere, from 0 to 5.
- Source count: how many places you saw it. You type it in; HQ does not count the links.
- Unanswered, Answered, Refresh due: whether content answering the pain exists yet. Refresh due means the answer needs a fresh version.
- Answered by: the content pieces that answer the pain, each with its stage. "A piece that no longer exists" means a linked piece was deleted.
- Product idea: the entry is flagged as something that could become a product.

## Good to know
- "Make this" creates the Content idea before the form opens. If you cancel the form, the idea stays in the brand's Content list; delete it there if you don't want it.
- "Make this" does not mark the pain as answered and does not add the new piece to "Answered by". Change the status with "Edit" once the answer is out.
- This screen has no control for filling in "Answered by". Not confirmed: what fills it.
- There is no delete button for entries on this screen.
- If none of your brands are switched on, the screen says "No internal brand projects are switched on yet."
