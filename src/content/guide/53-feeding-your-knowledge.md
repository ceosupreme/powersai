# Feeding your knowledge into the playbooks
<!-- part: Research and playbooks | order: 53 | slug: feeding-your-knowledge | route: /playbooks | tags: playbooks, add your material, sources, playbook inbox, drive, merge, courses, notes, research reports, youtube -->

Your own research, courses, videos and notes make the playbooks better than anything Claude finds on its own, and they always come first. You can hand material over in two ways: the "Add your material" button in HQ, or a file dropped in the Drive folder "Supreme Team HQ - Playbook Inbox". The nightly production run reads it and merges it into the right playbooks.

## Where to find it

- In HQ, open "Playbooks" in the menu under "Growth & Marketing". The "Add your material" button is at the top of the page, and again in the "Sources" box inside every playbook.
- In Google Drive, the folder is "Supreme Team HQ - Playbook Inbox". Items that have been merged move to the "Merged" folder inside it.

## How to use it

### Add material from HQ

1. Open "Playbooks".
2. Tap "Add your material". If you are already inside a playbook, use the button in its "Sources" box instead, and the material is tied to that playbook. The window then says "For:" and the playbook's name.
3. Pick the "Kind": "AI research report", "YouTube video", "X post", "Course", "My own notes", "Web article", "Book" or "Other".
4. Type a "Title (required)".
5. Add a "Link (optional)", paste text into "Notes or pasted text (optional)", or both.
6. When you opened the window from the top of the page, you can search for a "Playbook (optional)". Not sure which one? Leave it blank and Claude will route it.
7. Tap "Save". The window shows "Saved. Claude merges it on the next production run." Tap "Add another" for more, or "Close".

### Add material from Google Drive

1. Put the file in "Supreme Team HQ - Playbook Inbox". A Google Doc, a PDF, a text file, or a document that holds a YouTube or X link all work.
2. If the file is for one playbook, start its name with that playbook's name, for example "Pinterest - my notes". That routes it straight to the Pinterest playbook.
3. The next production run adds it to HQ as a source and works on it from there.

### See what happened to your material

1. Open "Playbooks" and open the playbook it was for.
2. Look at the "Sources" box. Each item shows its kind, the date you added it, and its status.
3. When it says "Merged into" a version, the two sentences under it say what was taken in, and what was left out and why.
4. The report email "Production run: what got made" also lists what was merged where, what was rejected and why, and how many items are still waiting.

## What happens after you add something

- The production run reads up to four new items a night, oldest first.
- When the playbook it informs is already written, your material is merged in that night as a new version. Small additions and corrections raise the version a little, for example 1.0 to 1.1. A change to the steps themselves raises it to the next whole number, for example 1.4 to 2.0. The new version's "What changed" list names your source.
- When the playbook is still planned, your material is marked "Assigned" and that playbook jumps to the front of the writing line. Up to two playbooks are written each night.
- Your material wins over outside opinions and over Claude's earlier text. The one exception is an official platform rule or a legal or safety requirement. Then the official rule stays in the steps, your point is kept as a "Your note" line, and the conflict comes to you as a decision in the email and as a Life Pulse card with an "Open playbook" button.
- A YouTube video is read from its title, description and transcript when they are free to read. If it cannot be read without paid credits, it stays "New" and the email names it.
- Nothing you add is ever deleted. Material that cannot be used is marked "Rejected" with the reason.

## Words on this screen

- "New": saved and waiting for the next production run.
- "Assigned": routed to a playbook that is still planned. It will be used when that playbook is written.
- "Merged into" a version: your material is in that version of the playbook.
- "Rejected": it could not be used. The reason is shown right after the word.
- "Sources": the list of everything that fed a playbook, yours and Claude's research.
- "Your note": a point of yours kept next to an official rule it disagrees with.

## Good to know

- One topic per item merges best. A long course is fine, but a title that names the platform, format or brand it is about helps it land in the right place.
- For a video, paste your own key points in "Notes or pasted text (optional)". Your notes are always readable, even when the transcript is not.
- When you disagree with a playbook, add "My own notes" saying so. Your notes beat everything except official platform rules and legal or safety requirements.
- The readable copy of every written playbook is saved as a Google Doc in the Drive folder "Supreme Team HQ - Playbooks", named with the shelf, the title and the version.
- Material goes to the playbooks only through these two doors. Pasting a long document into a chat with Claude also works, but ask Claude to file it as playbook material so it lands in the Sources list.
