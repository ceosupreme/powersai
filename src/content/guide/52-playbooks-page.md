# Playbooks

Playbooks is HQ's how-to library: the written instructions HQ and Claude follow whenever they make something, publish it, earn from it or promote it. Use this screen to see which playbooks are written, thin or still planned, to read one in full, and to hand Claude your own material so it is merged into the next version.

## Where to find it
- On a computer, tap "Playbooks" under "Content & Production" in the left menu.
- On a phone, tap "More" in the bottom bar, then "Playbooks" under "Content & Production".
- Playbook names on a brand's "Playbooks" and "Setup" tabs, and the playbook name on the page of a job that is writing a playbook, open this screen with that playbook already open.
- The menu shows it to admin accounts only. The old /methods address also opens it.

## What you see
- The heading "Playbooks", the "Add your material" button, and the link "Or drop files in the Playbook Inbox folder".
- A row of seven shelves that you can swipe sideways. Under each shelf name is a count such as "9 written, 2 thin, 4 planned". The screen opens on "Formats".
- "Formats" holds what you make, such as a long-form video, a printable or a book. "Platforms" holds where it goes live, such as YouTube, Etsy or KDP (Amazon's self-publishing service). "Money paths" holds how it earns, such as a lead magnet (a free item that collects contacts), affiliate links or a direct sale. "Niches" holds audience playbooks. "Promotion" holds ways to drive traffic. "Rules" holds platform requirements and each brand's rules. "Plays" holds proven combinations; it stays empty until a pipeline's 30-day check shows something worked, and it says so.
- A "Search by title" box and a brand filter synchronized with the main selector. On "Niches" there is also a group filter that starts on "All groups".
- The list for the chosen shelf. Each row shows the title, a status word, the version (such as v1.2), "Review by" with a date, and the Claude skill or pack that goes with it, or "No skill or pack".
- On "Niches", rows are grouped under Pets, Health and wellness, Safety and self-reliance, Money and business, Local and city, Music and nightlife, Faith and spirit, Lifestyle and hobbies, Cross-brand buyers, and Other. Each row lists the brands it serves, with "(core)" after its core brands.
- Tapping a row opens the playbook in a side panel. From the top: the status word, "Version" and "Review by"; the description and summary; an "Open readable copy" button when a readable copy exists; the numbered "Steps", "Setup" and "Adds to a pipeline" lists, where each step says "Who:" and, when there is one, "Check:"; for niches, "Brand angles", "Money map" and "Content map"; the "Full playbook" text in a scrolling box; "Being written or updated"; "Version history"; "Sources"; and, for admins, "Edit (admin)".

## How to use it
### Browse a shelf
1. Tap a shelf name in the shelf row.
2. Scroll the list and tap a row to open that playbook.
3. Close the panel to go back to the list.

### Find a specific playbook
1. Type part of its title in "Search by title". Only titles are searched.
2. To narrow the list to one brand, pick it in the brand filter. You see that brand's playbooks plus the company-wide ones that belong to no brand.
3. On "Niches", pick a group in the group filter.
4. If nothing fits, the list says "No playbooks match."

### See who is writing it and read older versions
1. Open the playbook.
2. Under "Being written or updated", tap "Pipeline" to open the job that is writing or updating it. If no job is, it says "No job is working on this playbook."
3. Under "Version history", tap "Open" beside an older version to read it. "Open now" marks the version on screen.

### Add your own material
1. Tap "Add your material" at the top of the screen, or the same button inside a playbook's "Sources" panel to attach the material to that playbook.
2. Pick a "Kind": "AI research report", "YouTube video", "X post", "Course", "My own notes", "Web article", "Book" or "Other".
3. Type a "Title (required)". Add a "Link (optional)" and any "Notes or pasted text (optional)".
4. If you started from the top button, you can choose a "Playbook (optional)" by searching all shelves. If you are not sure, leave it blank and Claude will route it.
5. Tap "Save". You see "Saved. Claude merges it on the next production run." Then tap "Add another" or "Close".
6. To send files instead, tap "Or drop files in the Playbook Inbox folder". It opens the Google Drive folder named "Supreme Team HQ - Playbook Inbox"; put the files there. You can also tell any Claude chat to add material to your playbooks.

### Fix a version number, review date or status (admin)
1. Open the playbook and scroll to "Edit (admin)".
2. Change "Version", "Review by", "Last reviewed" or "Status". The status choices are "Planned", "Active", "Under review", "Superseded" and "Archived".
3. Tap "Save".


### Find a playbook quickly
1. Choose a category. Category buttons wrap so every category stays reachable.
2. Search by title, then narrow by brand or readiness: Written, Thin or Planned.
3. Choose "Sort by" to order by title, put Written playbooks first, or show the earliest review date first. Missing review dates go last.
4. Use "Clear filters" to reset the search and filters.

## Words on this screen
- Playbook: written instructions for one way of making, publishing, earning or promoting.
- Shelf: one group of playbooks, such as "Formats" or "Rules".
- Pipeline: a production job in HQ, made of ordered steps that are built from playbooks.
- Planned: the playbook is not written yet.
- Thin: the playbook exists but is short, is past its review-by date, or is marked under review.
- Written: the playbook is complete and current. It is active, its review-by date has not passed, and it is deep enough: about 1,500 or more characters of full text, a linked Claude skill or pack, or at least five steps. Rules and Promotion playbooks are short by design, so they count as Written when they are active, current and carry their one-line instruction.
- Superseded and Archived: an older version that a newer one replaced, and a retired playbook. Both are hidden from the shelves but still listed in "Version history".
- Version: the playbook's version number, such as v1.0.
- Review by: the date the playbook should be checked again. After that date it shows as Thin until someone reviews it.
- Effective: the date a version took effect.
- Skill or pack: the Claude skill (a saved set of instructions Claude loads for one kind of work) or pack that goes with the playbook.
- Who: who does a step: "You", "Claude", or "Tool (you)" for a step you do with a tool.
- Check: how to tell a step was done right.
- Brand angles: the brands a niche serves. "Core" means the niche is central to that brand; "Angle" means the brand reaches the niche from one side, described under its name.
- Money map: the niche's ways to earn, ranked, with the reason for each.
- Content map: the formats planned for the niche, with series and level.
- Source status: "New" when saved, "Assigned" while it is being worked on, "Merged into" a version number once it has been used, or "Rejected:" followed by the reason.
- Job status: "Ready", "Running", "Blocked", "Waiting on a decision", "In review", "Finished" or "Parked".

## Good to know
- Each night a scheduled run writes planned playbooks, two at a time, starting with a first batch of twelve, and merges your new material into new versions.
- Your material outranks other sources. When it conflicts with an official platform rule, the conflict comes to you as a decision instead of being settled for you.
- Nothing you send is dropped silently. Every item ends as "Merged into" a version, with its merge notes shown under it, or "Rejected:" with a reason.
- The counts under each shelf name cover the whole shelf. They don't change when you search or filter.
- In "Edit (admin)", "Active" is not a word you see on the shelves. An active playbook shows as Written or Thin depending on its depth and review date, "Under review" shows as Thin, and choosing "Superseded" or "Archived" takes it off the shelves.
- When you create a pipeline, playbook choices that are not written yet end in "(planned)", and each one becomes a step that reads "Method not written yet". Niche playbooks add background to a pipeline but never add steps. Playbooks used to be called Methods, which is why that step still says Method.
- Jobs that write playbooks appear only here, under "Being written or updated", and not in a brand's job lists.
- If the library can't load, the screen says "Playbooks could not be loaded." with a "Try again" link.

The Brand filter stays in sync with the main header selector. Choose All brands to return to the full accessible list.
