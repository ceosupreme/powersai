# Outreach Templates
<!-- part: Tools and system | order: 84 | slug: outreach-templates | route: /templates | tags: outreach templates, templates, scripts, sales scripts, sms, email, dm, voicemail, fill in the blanks, copy, tokens, placeholders -->

Outreach Templates is a library of ready-made sales messages (texts, emails, direct messages and voicemail scripts) with blanks for names, links and numbers. You pick one, fill in the blanks and copy the finished text to send from your own phone or email. Nothing is sent from HQ.

## Where to find it

- On a computer, open the "Tools" group in the side menu and choose "Outreach Templates". On a phone, tap "More", then "Templates" under "Tools".
- To add or change templates, open "Admin Panel" in the "System" group (called "Settings" on the phone), then the "Settings" tab, then "Outreach Templates". On a phone those tabs show icons only, so editing is easier on a computer.

## What you see

- A search box ("Search name or body…") and an industry menu ("All verticals", "Universal" or one industry).
- Category tabs: "All", plus one tab per category.
- A card per template: its name, a channel badge ("SMS", "Email", "DM" or "Voicemail"), its category and industry, and the first line of the message.
- Tapping a card opens the fill-in window: "Fill in the blanks" boxes (each with a label, an example and sometimes a hint), a "Subject preview" for emails, a "Body preview" with unfilled blanks highlighted, and the buttons "Copy subject" (emails only) and "Copy body".

## How to use it

### Send a message from a template
1. Search, or pick an industry and a category tab.
2. Tap the template's card.
3. Fill in each box. The preview updates as you type.
4. Tap "Copy body", and "Copy subject" for emails.
5. Paste into your text, email or direct-message app and send it yourself.

### Add or change a template
1. Open "Admin Panel", then the "Settings" tab, then "Outreach Templates".
2. Tap "New template". A blank SMS template named "New template" appears in the category "uncategorized".
3. Set the name, the category (type a new one to create it), the channel, the "Active" switch, the industry (leave it blank for universal), the subject for emails and the body.
4. In the body, write each blank as a word inside double curly braces, for example {{first}} for the person's first name or {{biz}} for the business name.
5. Tap "Save". Use "Duplicate" to copy a template or "Delete" to remove it.

## Words on this screen

- Template: a saved message with blanks to fill in.
- Channel: "SMS" (text), "Email", "DM" (direct message on social media) or "Voicemail" (a script to read).
- Category: the purpose of the template. Each category gets its own tab.
- Universal: works for any industry.
- Blank: a placeholder in double curly braces that the fill-in window turns into a box. The labels you will see include "Their first name", "Business name", "Monthly leak amount ($)", "Your Loom video link", "Booking link", "Qualifier link", "Checkup link" and "Time window".
- Active, Inactive: only active templates appear on this page.

## Good to know

- If you copy while blanks are still empty, they come out as bracketed labels such as "[business name]" and a warning appears. Replace them before sending.
- What you type in the boxes is not saved; it clears when you close the window.
- Templates belong to your whole account; the project picker does not filter them.
- The monthly leak amount is meant to come from a prospect's checkup in the Prospect Dock or a Leak Stack run.
- These templates are separate from the AI "Outreach Drafts" on a CRM company and the "Draft" button in the Prospect Dock.
