# Getting around HQ

Supreme Team HQ is the private back office of the Supreme Team Media site. This article covers signing in, the side menu on a computer, the bottom bar and "More" sheet on a phone, the header bar at the top of every screen, and how to choose which project or brand the screens show. Every other article assumes you know these controls.

## Where to find it

- Sign in at /login (the address /auth opens the same page). If you open an HQ screen while signed out, HQ asks you to sign in and then takes you to that screen.
- The menus and header appear on every HQ screen once you are signed in. Your home screen after signing in is Portfolio.

## What you see

The sign-in page shows a card titled "Welcome back" with "Email" and "Password" fields, an eye icon that reveals the password, a "Forgot password?" link and the "Sign In" button. There is no sign-up option; an admin creates new accounts.

On a computer, the side menu runs down the left in six groups:

- "Workspace": "Today", "Portfolio", "Weekly Review", "Insights", "Team".
- "Clients & Leads": "CRM", "Capture Inbox", "Automation Inbox", "Reactivation", "Recovery Reports".
- "Growth & Marketing": "Growth Audit", "Leak Stack", "Prospect Dock", "Foundation Audit", "Marketing Hub", "Content", "Radar", "Playbooks", "Production Board", "Answer Bank", "Channel Revenue".
- "Brand & Assets": "Brand Kit", "Offers", "Products", "Affiliate Programs".
- "Tools": "Tasks", "Logs", "Chat", "Outreach Templates".
- "System": "Guide", "Launch Checklist", "Admin Panel".

Below the groups sit "Preview Role", your name with a small role tag, and "Collapse".

On a phone (any screen narrower than 768 pixels), a bottom bar replaces the side menu: "Today", "Portfolio", "Inbox" and "More". "More" opens a sheet with the same groups as tiles, minus those three screens. In the sheet, "Outreach Templates" is called "Templates", "Admin Panel" is called "Settings", and "Sign Out" sits at the bottom.

The header bar holds, from left to right: the project picker, the week picker (only on Today, Portfolio, Weekly Review and a few older screens such as Dashboard), "New Task" (a plus sign on a phone), a magnifying glass for search, a sun or moon icon for the theme, and an inbox icon for quick capture. A round lightbulb button, "Ask Supreme", floats at the bottom right and opens a chat panel about the selected project.

## How to use it

### To sign in

1. Go to /login and type your email and password (at least 6 characters). Problems show in red under the field.
2. Tap "Sign In". You land on the screen you were trying to open, or on Portfolio.

### To reset a forgotten password

1. Tap "Forgot password?", enter your email and tap "Send reset link". "Check your email" appears either way.
2. Open the link in the email, type a "New password" of at least 8 characters, repeat it in "Confirm password" and tap "Update password".
3. If the page says "Link expired", tap "Reset password" to ask for a new link.

### To move between screens

1. On a computer, click an item in the side menu. "Collapse" (or Ctrl+B, Cmd+B on a Mac) shrinks the menu to icons; hover an icon to see its name.
2. On a phone, tap a button in the bottom bar, or tap "More" and then a tile.

### To choose the project or brand you are working on

1. Tap the project picker at the left of the header. It shows the current project's name, or "All projects".
2. Choose a project to point most screens at it, or "All projects" for the cross-brand lists on Today.
3. Opening a brand from Portfolio, or choosing a project in the Insights filter, also changes the picker.

### To capture an idea fast

1. Tap the inbox icon in the header (or press Ctrl+Shift+K).
2. Type the idea, task or note and tap "Save". A "Captured" message appears with an "Inbox" button.

### To sign out

1. On a computer, click your name at the bottom of the side menu and choose "Sign out".
2. On a phone, tap "More", then "Sign Out".

## Words on this screen

- Project: any brand, client or prospect HQ tracks. Most screens show only the selected project.
- "All projects": no single project is selected, so cross-brand views appear.
- "Money Lanes": the name "Foundation Audit" takes in the menu when the selected project is one of your own brands.
- Tasks badge: a red number on "Tasks" counting tasks that are not done and are assigned to you or past due for anyone. It refreshes every minute.
- Chat badge: a red number on "Chat" counting unread messages from other people in your chat channels. It refreshes every 30 seconds.
- Admin-only items: "Playbooks", "Production Board", "Answer Bank", "Admin Panel", "Preview Role", and "Settings" in your name menu. Only accounts with the admin role see them; your owner account has that role.
- "Preview Role": shows the menus and layout another role would see ("Owner", "General Manager", "Lead", "Producer", "Contributor"). A yellow "Previewing as" bar with "Exit Preview" appears. Data still loads under your own account, and reloading the page ends the preview.

## Good to know

- HQ remembers your project choice in this browser. After you sign out and back in, it selects your first project alphabetically, so choose "All projects" again for the cross-brand lists.
- The project picker appears only when you have more than one project, is hidden on Portfolio, and never lists prospects; they live on the Prospect Dock.
- The week picker lists completed weeks only and starts on the most recent one.
- The Tasks and Chat badges appear only in the side menu on a computer; the phone "More" sheet has none.
- The header search is a short jump list of older screens ("Dashboard", "Revenue", "Labor", "Delivery", "Client Experience" and a few more) plus "New Task" and "New Log". It does not search your data and leaves out most HQ screens.
- Opening a client from Portfolio's "Clients" tab switches HQ into a client layout: "← Portfolio" appears in the header, the phone bottom bar changes to "Review", "Insights", "Tasks", "Chat" and "More", and a "Daily Log" button appears on a computer. Tap "← Portfolio" to return.
- Your theme choice ("Light", "Dark" or "System") is saved to your account and follows you to other devices.
- "Privacy", "Terms" and "Support" under the sign-in card are not connected to anything.
- Not confirmed: what data "Ask Supreme" uses for your own brands; it was built for the client scorecards.
