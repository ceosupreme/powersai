# Offers
<!-- part: Money and offers | order: 62 | slug: offers | route: /offers | tags: offers, services, service library, pricing, starter price, premium price, deliverables, timeline, best target, lead matching, analyze lead, outreach, crm -->

Offers is your library of services: what each one is, who it is for, what it delivers, how long it takes and what it costs. The AI in the CRM (HQ's client and lead tracker) uses your active offers when it analyzes a lead and drafts outreach, so keep this list current.

## Where to find it
- On a computer, tap "Offers" under "Brand & Assets" in the left menu.
- On a phone, tap "More" in the bottom bar, then "Offers" under "Brand & Assets".
- It is one list for your whole account. The project picker at the top of the screen does not change it.

## What you see
- The heading "Offers", the line "Your service library — used by Client Acquisition AI to match leads.", and a "New Offer" button.
- A table with "Name", "Who it's for", "Timeline", "Starter", "Premium", "Status", and edit and delete buttons. On a phone, swipe it sideways.
- With no offers yet, the table says "No offers yet."

## How to use it
### Add an offer
1. Tap "New Offer".
2. Type the "Name". It is the only required field.
3. Pick a "Status": "active" or "draft". New offers start as active.
4. Fill in "Description", "Who it's for", "Problem solved", "Deliverables", "Timeline" (for example 2-4 weeks), "Starter price ($)", "Premium price ($)" and "Best target".
5. Tap "Add". An "Offer added" message confirms it.

### Change an offer or take it out of use
1. Tap the pencil on the offer's row, change what you need, and tap "Save".
2. To stop the AI from recommending an offer without deleting it, set its "Status" to "draft".

### Delete an offer
1. Tap the trash button on the offer's row.
2. Confirm "Delete this offer?".

## Words on this screen
- Offer: one service you sell.
- Who it's for: the kind of client the offer suits.
- Problem solved: the problem the offer fixes for that client.
- Deliverables: what the client receives.
- Timeline: how long the work takes.
- Starter and Premium: the entry price and the top price for the offer.
- Best target: the ideal client to aim the offer at.
- active and draft: active offers are used by the CRM's AI; draft offers are kept but ignored.

## Good to know
- In the CRM, "Analyze Lead" reads the name, "Who it's for", "Problem solved" and "Best target" of every active offer and recommends one for the lead. "Draft outreach" then uses the full details of the recommended offer, including its deliverables, timeline and prices.
- This list is separate from the public Packages page on your website, which reads its own package and service records. Changing an offer here does not change the website.
- The Foundation Audit item "At least one service offer" does not read this list. It checks the packages added in the "Current packages" step of the project's "Setup wizard", even though its suggested fix says to define a service offer in Offers.
- "Description", "Problem solved", "Deliverables" and "Best target" don't show in the table; open the offer to see them.
