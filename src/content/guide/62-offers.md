# Offers
<!-- part: Money and offers | order: 62 | slug: offers | route: /offers | tags: offers, services, service library, pricing, starter price, premium price, deliverables, timeline, best target, lead matching, analyze lead, outreach, crm -->

Offers describes services, audiences, deliverables, timing, and pricing. Offers can belong to a brand or client, or remain in the shared library.

## Where to find it
Open Brand & Assets, then Offers. The page follows the brand or client selected in the main header.

## How to use it
1. Choose a brand or client in the main selector. Choose All brands & clients to see the whole accessible library.
2. Search by offer, description, or audience. Sort by name, newest, or highest starter price.
3. Select New offer. Choose its Brand or client project; the current selection is filled in for you. Choose Shared library when it belongs to no particular brand.
4. Enter the name, active or draft status, audience, deliverables, timeline, and optional prices. Save.
5. Use the row's Edit button to change an offer or reassign it. Delete asks for confirmation.

## Existing offers
Offers created before brand assignment was available remain in Shared library. Use View shared library to find them, then Edit to assign each one. HQ never guesses which brand owns an existing offer. The shared-library heading makes this temporary view explicit even while a brand remains selected in the header.

## Good to know
The lead-analysis offer picker can still use the accessible service library. Assigning an offer changes where it appears in Offers; it does not duplicate it. If a project is removed, its offers are preserved without a brand assignment. A loading error is shown with Try again rather than an empty table.
