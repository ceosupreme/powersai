# Chat
<!-- part: Tools and system | order: 87 | slug: chat | route: /chat | tags: chat, messages, channels, direct message, dm, general, unread, badge, mention, team, conversation -->

Chat is HQ's built-in team messenger, with team channels and one-to-one direct messages. Use it to message anyone who has an HQ login, such as an assistant or a staff member, without leaving HQ. The Chat badge shows how many messages from other people you have not read yet.

## Where to find it

- On a computer: in the side menu, under "Tools", tap "Chat". The red number next to it is the Chat badge.
- On a phone: tap "More" in the bottom bar, then "Chat" under "Tools".
- From search: tap the magnifying glass and pick "Chat".
- Shift leads and staff use their own chat screen, described in "Roles, role preview and staff screens".

## What you see

- A list headed "Messages" with a "Search conversations..." box, a "Channels" section and a "Direct Messages" section. Each section has a "+" button. Conversations with unread messages show in bold with a count.
- The open conversation. Its header shows the channel name, its topic and the number of members, or, for a direct message, the other person's name and email address. Messages are grouped under "Dashboard", "Yesterday" or a full date, each with the sender's name and the time. Tagged names are highlighted, and changed messages say "(edited)".
- A message box that says "Type a message... (⌘+Enter to send)" and a send button.
- On a phone you see the list first. Tap a conversation to open it, and tap the back arrow to return to the list.
- The Chat badge counts unread messages from other people in every conversation you belong to. It refreshes every 30 seconds, and opening a conversation marks it as read.

## How to use it

### Send a message
1. Tap a channel or a person in the list.
2. Type in the message box. To tag someone, type @ and pick the name.
3. Tap the send button (or press Ctrl+Enter, Command+Enter on a Mac).

### Start a direct message
1. Tap the "+" next to "Direct Messages".
2. In "Start a Conversation", type in "Search team members..." and tap the person.
3. You see "Conversation started". The conversation appears under "Direct Messages"; tap it to open it. If you already had one with that person, HQ reuses it.

### Create a channel
1. Choose a single project in the project picker first, because a channel belongs to a project. With "All projects" chosen you get "Failed to create channel".
2. Tap the "+" next to "Channels".
3. Type a "Channel Name". It becomes lowercase and spaces turn into dashes. Add a "Topic (optional)" if you like.
4. Tap "Create Channel". You see "Channel created". You are its only member, and there is no screen for adding people.

### Edit or delete your own message
1. On a computer, point at your message and click the three-dot button that appears. Not confirmed: on a phone the button may only appear after you tap the message.
2. Choose "Edit", change the text, then tap "Save" (or press Enter). "Cancel" or the Esc key leaves it unchanged.
3. Choose "Delete" to remove the message. It is removed at once, with no confirmation.

## Words on this screen

- Channel: a group conversation that belongs to one project.
- General: each project's default channel. HQ creates it, or adds you to it, the first time you open Chat with no conversations at all.
- Direct message: a private conversation between you and one other person.
- Unread: messages from other people that arrived after you last opened that conversation.
- Mention: an @name tag in a message. It highlights the name but sends no notification.

## Good to know

- Channels are shown according to membership and database access rules; their names do not control visibility.
- A conversation loads its latest 100 messages, displayed oldest to newest within that recent window. Older history is outside this initial window.
- New messages appear live in the open conversation. The lists refresh every 30 seconds.
- "Start a Conversation" also lists a few names from an older Asana team who have no HQ login. Picking one shows "Failed to start conversation".
- The green dot on a direct-message picture is decoration, not a real online status.
- There is no way to leave, rename or delete a channel.
- The owner's phone menu shows no Chat badge. Managers and staff see it on their bottom bar.
- The page switch for this screen is called "Chat" in Admin Panel, "Permissions".
