# Weekly Review

Weekly Review is the one-week scorecard for a client project that sends point-of-sale data: the overall score and grade, four pillar scores, headline numbers, task and team performance, wins, warnings and a short action plan you can approve. Use it to prepare for a weekly client meeting. Your own brands do not have this scorecard, so for them it shows "No weekly data available".

## Where to find it

- Side menu: "Workspace", then "Weekly Review". On a phone, tap "More", then "Weekly Review"; in the client layout it is "Review" in the bottom bar. The address is /weekly-review.
- It shows the project and the week chosen in the header pickers.

## What you see

When the selected project has a scorecard for the week:

- Warning bars when needed: "Incomplete week" (some daily data has not synced, so totals are partial) and "Limited Data" (no point-of-sale sales that week, so revenue and guest numbers may show "N/A").
- The heading "Weekly Review", the line "Week of" with the dates and project name, and a "Share" button.
- A score panel: the overall score out of 100, its letter grade, the change from the week before (such as "+3 pts"), boxes for "Revenue", "Labor", "Operations" and "Guest Exp" with grades and changes, and a summary line such as "Scored 82/100 (B) — Revenue +4 pts, Labor -3 pts."
- Six headline numbers: "Net Sales", "Labor %", "SPLH", "Tips %", "Turn Time" and "Guests", each compared with last year ("vs LY") or with a target when one exists.
- "Task Performance" ("GM tasks only"), showing "Resolution" and "In the Red"; tap it to open "Total Assigned", "Outstanding", "Completed This Week", "Resolution %", "On-Time %" and a trend chart against an 80 percent target, plus a short written brief. Without task data it shows a note instead, such as "no GM Asana tasks tracked for this project this week" or "GM not mapped for this venue".
- "Team Performance", showing "Violations" and "Exposure", or "no active employees tracked for this project this week".
- "Wins" and "Warnings" side by side. Up to three warnings appear; tap one to read more.
- "Action Plan" with up to five proposed actions for the week.
- "Performance by Pillar": four cards you can open for the metrics behind each pillar, and a "Show all pillar insights" switch.
- "12-Week Trends" (closed until tapped) and "Daily Sales & Labor %" for the week.

When there is no scorecard, you see only "No weekly data available" with "Select a bar and week to view your weekly review scorecard."

## How to use it

### To review a week

1. In the header, choose the client in the project picker.
2. Choose the week in the week picker. Only completed weeks are listed.
3. Read from the score panel down.

### To approve an action from the Action Plan

1. Tap the action to open it. You see "What Happened", "Evidence", "What 'done' looks like" and an "Asana Preview".
2. Tap "Approve & Push" (or "Approve & Push to Asana" inside the preview). HQ creates a task on the Tasks screen, plus an Asana task when Asana is set up for that client. When the list refreshes, the action leaves the Action Plan; it then appears under "Approved Tasks" on Insights.
3. To drop an action instead, open it and tap "Dismiss".

### To add a follow-up task

1. Tap "Related" on an action row.
2. Fill in the "Create Task" form, which starts linked to that action.

### To share the review

1. Tap "Share".
2. Choose "Copy for Meeting" to copy a text summary (pillar scores, wins, warnings, focus actions and praise lines), "Print" to print the page, or "Email" to open your mail app with the summary.

## Words on this screen

- Score and grade: the week's overall score out of 100; A is 90 and up, B 80 to 89, C 70 to 79, D 60 to 69, F below 60.
- "pts": points of change from the week before.
- "vs LY": compared with the same week last year.
- "SPLH": sales per labor hour.
- "Turn Time": average minutes a table or order takes.
- "Resolution": the share of the general manager's tasks that got resolved.
- "In the Red": Not confirmed which tasks count; its tooltip says it is a snapshot taken when the Monday scores are computed.
- "Violations": labor compliance alerts such as late or missed meal breaks, overtime and missing clock-outs.
- "Exposure": the estimated premium pay owed for missed meal breaks.
- "Approve & Push": approve the action and turn it into a task.

## Good to know

- This screen reads only the client weekly scorecard, which is built each week from point-of-sale, scheduling (7shifts) and Asana data and needs nearly a full week of daily sales. Your brands' scores live on Portfolio and in each brand workspace, and their weekly numbers are entered from Next 10's KPI rows or the brand's own page.
- The "Suggested next steps" panel can say a brand has "no Weekly Review this week" and link here, but this screen has no way to grade a brand.
- The Action Plan lists only proposed actions dated in the chosen week; "View all on Insights page →" appears when there are more than five.
- The "Assignee" menu in the Asana preview lists a fixed set of names carried over from the earlier bar client, and the name you pick is not passed on: the task is created without an assignee.
- The written briefs under "Task Performance" and "Team Performance" are written by AI once per week and then reused.
- The "Daily Sales & Labor %" chart never shows its "Labor exceeded target" line, because no labor target is passed to it.
- The empty message still says "Select a bar", wording left from the earlier bar version.
