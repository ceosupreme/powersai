# Avenues tab
<!-- part: Running a brand | order: 24 | slug: avenues-tab | route: /project/:venueId | tags: avenues, money avenues, money lanes, lanes, turn this on, mark on, per hour, 90 days, foundation audit, severity, evidence -->

The "Avenues" tab shows the brand's money avenues: the ways this brand can earn, such as a store, a marketplace or a channel. It sorts them into those that are on, those not on yet, and those whose state is unknown, and shows what each is estimated to be worth. Use it to decide which avenue to switch on next and to start that work with "Turn this on".

## Where to find it

- Open the brand, then tap "Avenues" in the tab row.
- The address ends in ?tab=avenues.

## What you see

- Three groups, each with its count: "On", "Not on yet" and "Unknown". A group with nothing in it says "None."
- One card per avenue, showing:
  - Its name and a severity badge: "Low", "Medium", "High" or "Critical".
  - A dollar figure over "per hour": the estimated value of each hour of your time spent switching it on.
  - A dollar figure over "est. 90 days": the estimated money it could bring in over 90 days.
  - The recommended fix, when there is one.
  - "Status:" and the avenue's current state: "Satisfied", "Missing", "Partial", "Unknown" or "Not Applicable".
  - On avenues that are not on: "Mark on" (admins only) and "Turn this on".
- A "Setup" list at the bottom: the brand's other Foundation Audit items, meaning its setup checks that are not money avenues, each with its status. This list is for reading only.
- If the brand has no Foundation Audit items at all, the tab says "No Foundation Audit items for this brand yet."

## How to use it

### To switch an avenue on with a pipeline
1. Find the avenue under "Not on yet" or "Unknown".
2. Tap "Turn this on". The "Create pipeline" form opens with the title "Turn on:" and the avenue's name.
3. Change the title, niche, level or money destination if needed, then tap "Create pipeline".
4. The new pipeline page opens. The job has five fixed steps: set up the accounts this avenue needs (you), the first product or piece for it (Claude), list or publish it (you), a promotion plan of three pieces pointing at it (Claude), and a 30-day check of what sold or ranked (Claude).

### To mark an avenue as on
1. Tap "Mark on" on its card.
2. Paste a link that proves it is live, such as the store page, into "Evidence URL (optional)", or leave it empty.
3. Tap "Save". HQ shows "Marked on" and the card moves to "On". "Cancel" closes the box without saving.

## Words on this screen

- Avenue: a way the brand earns money, such as an Etsy shop, Amazon KDP books or YouTube. HQ also calls these money lanes.
- "On": the avenue is set up and running. Its status reads "Satisfied".
- "Not on yet": the avenue is not set up. Its status reads "Missing".
- "Unknown": every other state: "Partial" (partly on), "Not Applicable", or "Unknown" (never checked). The card's "Status:" line says which.
- Severity: how much the avenue matters to the brand, from "Low" to "Critical".
- "per hour": the 90-day estimate, times how confident the estimate is, times its leverage, divided by the hours it takes you. It shows a dash when any of those numbers is missing.
- "est. 90 days": the estimated money over the next 90 days once the avenue is on.
- Foundation Audit: HQ's checklist of what a brand should have in place. Avenues are the checklist items that earn money.
- Evidence URL: a link that shows the avenue is live.

## Good to know

- The first step of a "Turn this on" job lists, in its instructions, the brand's setup items that are not on yet, so you can see what has to be fixed first.
- HQ matches the job to a platform playbook by the avenue's name (Etsy, KDP, YouTube, Pinterest, Payhip, Gumroad, Shopify, TikTok, Instagram, Facebook, blog or email), when that playbook exists.
- Repeating "Turn this on" for the same avenue opens its existing active pipeline. Finished and canceled jobs remain in history.
- Not confirmed: whether finishing the job marks the avenue on by itself. If an avenue is live and still shows under "Not on yet", use "Mark on".
- Avenues that are not on and rated critical or high also show in "Next 10", with their value per hour, for example "about $40/hr".
- The "Playbooks" tab calls a partly-on avenue "Partly on", while this tab files it under "Unknown".
- There is no way to change the estimates on this tab. Not confirmed: where they are set; no screen covered in this guide edits them.

Admins can use "Estimates" on an avenue to edit estimated 90-day revenue, confidence (0 to 1), leverage and hours. These are planning estimates, not recorded earnings.
