# Overview tab
<!-- part: Running a brand | order: 21 | slug: overview-tab | route: /project/:venueId | tags: overview, brand numbers, overall score, revenue this month, list size, followers, published this month, waiting on you, next 10, activity, pillars, scores, download pdf -->

The "Overview" tab is the first thing you see when you open a brand. It shows the brand's headline numbers, everything waiting on you for this brand, the brand's Next 10, its scores and its recent activity. Use it for a quick check-in and to clear small tasks without leaving the brand.

## Where to find it

- Open a brand from "Portfolio" under "My brands". It opens on "Overview".
- From any other tab, tap "Overview" at the start of the tab row.

## What you see

From top to bottom:
- A setup line, for example "Setup: 6 of 14 in place, 8 to set up." with an "Open Setup" link. It only appears when the brand's setup map lists items the brand needs.
- Six large number tiles. A dash means there is no number yet.
  - "Overall score": the same weighted score for the current week that the brand's Portfolio card shows.
  - "Revenue this month": the total of this month's revenue entries for the brand.
  - "List size": the newest weekly number entered for a KPI (a number you track each week) whose name mentions email or list.
  - "Followers": the follower counts on the brand's channel records, added together.
  - "Published this month": placements (one piece put on one channel) that are marked published and were verified during this calendar month.
  - "Waiting on you": how many items are in the list below.
- "Waiting on you": your open steps for this brand and its content that is ready for approval.
  - A step shows its title, a priority badge ("P1" to "P4", or "No priority") and a "Step" badge, with a "Done" button. Steps that belong to a pipeline also have an "Open" button.
  - A content piece shows "Ready to approve" and its "Quality" score, with a "Done" button that approves it and an "Open" button that opens the "Content" tab.
  - When there is nothing, it says "Nothing is waiting on you for this brand."
- "Next 10": the ranked list of the next ten things to do for this brand, with a "Download PDF" button. A row that belongs to a pipeline ends in "· Pipeline" and opens that pipeline when tapped.
- The score section: "Overall (weighted)", one card per pillar (a scored area of the brand), and short one-glance sections such as "Action plan", "Insights", "Products by stage", "Content pipeline", "Revenue this month" and "Score trend (12 weeks)".
- "Activity": the last ten things that happened, newest first, each with how long ago. Examples are "Done:" and a step, "Job created:" and a title, "Published on" a channel, "Listed on" an outlet, and research runs. When empty it says "No activity recorded yet."
- A "Setup" button at the very bottom. It unfolds the older project tools: a "Continue setup" card when go-live steps remain, "Capture link", "Email delivery", "Proposals", and shortcuts to "Weekly Review", "Growth Audit", "Automation Inbox", "Recovery Reports" and "Brand Vault".

## How to use it

### To finish one of your steps
1. Find the step in "Waiting on you".
2. Tap "Done". HQ shows "Marked done" and the step leaves the list.
3. If the step belongs to a pipeline, HQ moves that pipeline to its next step by itself.

### To approve a content piece
1. Find a row with the "Ready to approve" badge.
2. To look at it first, tap "Open". The "Content" tab opens.
3. Tap "Done" on the row to approve it. HQ shows "Approved".

### To open the pipeline behind a step
1. Tap "Open" on the step in "Waiting on you", or tap a "Next 10" row that ends in "· Pipeline".

### To save the Next 10 as a checklist
1. Tap "Download PDF" in the "Next 10" card. HQ saves a PDF checklist named after the brand.

## Words on this screen

- "Overall score": the brand's weighted score for the current week, built from its pillar scores.
- Pillar: one scored area of the brand. Each pillar has a weight, and the overall score is the weighted average of the pillars that have a score.
- "Waiting on you": open steps that are yours to do for this brand, plus content ready for your approval.
- "P1" to "P4": priority, from P1 (most urgent) to P4 (low).
- "Step": a single task. It may stand alone or belong to a pipeline.
- "Ready to approve": a piece with a quality score of 80 or more, no hard fails, and no approval yet.

## Good to know

- The "Done" button on a content row approves the piece. It does not publish or post it. Nothing in HQ posts to any platform by itself.
- Claude's steps never appear in "Waiting on you"; Claude does them in its nightly run. A step that waits on an earlier step stays out of the list until that step is done. Snoozed and rejected steps are left out too.
- The setup score from the "Setup" tab makes up 20 percent of the overall score. The copy of it used here refreshes nightly, so a setup change can take until the next morning to show in "Overall score".
- The "Overall score" tile and "Overall (weighted)" in the score section are worked out separately but from the same week and weights, so they normally match. Right after you enter a KPI number, "Overall (weighted)" updates first and the tile catches up when the saved pillar score refreshes.
- The "Setup" button at the bottom holds older project tools. It is not the "Setup" tab, which is the brand's account map.
