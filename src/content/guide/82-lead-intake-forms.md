# Lead Intake Forms
<!-- part: Clients and leads | order: 82 | slug: lead-intake-forms | route: none | tags: lead forms, contact form, inquiry, free audit, free checkup, qualifier, voice, chat, capture link, packages, industry pages, inbound, owner alert, thank-you email, follow-up, emergency -->

Several public pages collect leads: the contact form on the main site pages, the package builder, the industry pages, the free Google checkup, the lead qualifier and each client's capture link. Every one of them saves the lead into the CRM's "Inbound" tab, and then, depending on the source, emails you, sends the visitor a thank-you email or queues follow-up drafts for approval. This article explains where each kind of lead lands and what happens next.

## Where to find it

- Every lead lands in "CRM", on the "Inbound" tab, under "New". On a phone, tap "More", then "CRM".
- The public sources: the contact section near the bottom of most site pages, the package builder on /pricing, the industry pages under /for/, the free checkup at /free-audit, the lead qualifier under /qualify/, each client's capture link under /q/, and proposal pages under /proposal/.

## What you see

- Main contact form (home, services, about, areas, work, hire, startups and pricing pages): name, business, email and message, with "Interested in", "Budget range" and "Timing" lines added to the message when chosen.
- Package builder: the message lists the starter package and the services picked.
- Industry pages: name, email, business and note. The areas of need the visitor ticked are saved but not shown on the card.
- Free checkup: a lead is created only when the visitor adds an email to unlock the full report. The card shows "Ready" and a summary of what the checkup found.
- Lead qualifier: the visitor talks or types through "Voice", "Chat" or "Form". The card shows the industry and, when asked, an urgency badge. "Ready" appears only for voice or chat leads the AI judged qualified.
- Client capture link: the same qualifier, but the lead is credited to that client.
- Proposal requests arrive with the type "agreement_request", and Prospect Dock promotions arrive already under "Promoted".

## How to use it

### Respond to a new lead
1. Watch for the email alert titled "New website inquiry" with the person's name. It lists the phone, email, company, interests, budget, timing, channel and source, and links to the CRM.
2. Open "CRM", then "Inbound", then "New".
3. Contact the lead, then tap "Mark responded". If the button is missing, either the lead has no urgency yet (choose one under "Set urgency:") or it already shows "Responded", often because the thank-you email went out.
4. Tap "Promote to CRM", "Create project from lead", "Mark reviewed" or "Archive".

### Test a form
1. Open the public page in a private browser window and send test details with an email address you control.
2. Check that the lead appears under "Inbound" and that the alert email arrives.
3. Archive the test lead.

## Words on this screen

- Inbound lead: a submission saved from any public form.
- Source: where the lead came from, such as the public site, a qualifier, the free checkup or the prospect miner. It appears in the alert email.
- Ready: the lead met the industry's definition of qualified, or unlocked the free checkup.
- Urgency: how soon the visitor needs help, from "EMERGENCY" to "Maintenance".
- Capture link: a client's own qualifier link. Leads from it belong to that client.
- Owner alert: the "New website inquiry" email sent to you.
- Thank-you email: an automatic note to the visitor that their message arrived.
- Follow-up sequence: AI-drafted follow-ups for a client's lead, waiting in the Automation Inbox for approval.

## Good to know

- You get an owner alert for every lead that is not tied to a client: the main site, packages, industry pages, the qualifier, free checkup unlocks and proposal requests.
- Leads from a client's capture link send you no alert. If the client's "Follow-up sequences" automation is on, follow-up drafts are queued in the Automation Inbox instead.
- The visitor gets a thank-you email when they gave an email address and the lead is not tied to a client, except free checkup unlocks and proposal requests. It goes out in Spanish when the visitor used a Spanish page, and sending it marks the lead as responded.
- An emergency lead on a client's capture link sends an alert to that client's main contact. Without a client, the alert is only logged, and text alerts are only logged for now too, so do not count on them.
- Replies to the hello mailbox appear in the Capture Inbox's "Email" tab, are forwarded to you and stop that lead's waiting follow-ups.
- Every form needs an email or a phone number. Spam bots that fill a hidden field are dropped quietly, and repeated sends from the same visitor are slowed down.
- Inbound cards do not show the phone number, the conversation transcript or the qualifier answers, so keep the alert email.
- The alert's link opens the CRM on the "Pipeline" tab. Switch to "Inbound".
