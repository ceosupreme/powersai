# Leads

The Leads page holds every lead from every source: businesses found on Google, people found on Facebook and Instagram, people who fill in a form on your website, and leads you add by hand. Each lead has a stage, an owner, a follow-up date and a record of every call, text and message. Use it to work your leads by hand every day.

## Where to find it

- On a computer, open "Growth & Marketing" in the side menu and choose "Leads". On a phone, tap "More", then "Leads".
- It covers your whole account, not one project, so the header picker does not change it.
- Managers and staff can open it only when you switch "Leads" on for their role or for that person in the Admin Panel menu switches. They see every lead, but the "Assigned to" filter starts on "Mine". Everyone on the team sees the name of the person a lead is assigned to.

## What you see

- "Export" and "Add lead" buttons at the top.
- "Find leads on Google", closed by default. Open it to use the "Mine prospects" card and see the last run.
- A search box (name, contact, city and what they said), "List" and "Board" buttons, and filters for stage, source, offer, assigned person and outreach (Manual or Auto). A line shows how many leads are showing.
- Stages, in order: New, Contacted, Replied, Meeting booked, Won, Dead. Leads already sent to the CRM carry an "In CRM" badge. A lead that must not be contacted carries a red "Do not contact" badge, or "Opted out" when the person asked to stop themselves.
- List: one row per lead with name, stage, source (Google, Facebook, Instagram, Website, Manual and others), offer, city, assigned person and next follow-up (red when overdue), plus call, email and "open post" buttons when we have them. The call and email buttons are hidden on a do-not-contact lead. Best fit comes first, then the biggest estimated leak, then the newest.
- Board: one column per stage with a count. On a computer, drag a card to another column. On a phone, scroll sideways and use "Move to" on the card.

## How to use it

### Work a lead
1. Tap a lead to open everything we know about it.
2. Change the stage from the menu under the name.
3. After you reach out, tap "Called", "Texted", "Emailed", "Messaged" or "Met", type an optional one-line summary and tap "Save". This records the contact and moves a New lead to Contacted. "Add note" records a note without counting as contact.
4. Edit the "Drafted message" and tap "Copy" to paste it where you send it. Tap "Save message" to keep changes.
5. Set "Assigned to", "Next follow-up" and "Notes".
6. The "Outreach" switch marks a lead Manual or Auto. Auto hands it to the outreach sequence, but no sequence is connected yet, so nothing is sent.
7. Switch on "Do not contact" when someone asks you to stop. A red notice appears at the top of the lead, the call and email buttons go away, "Outreach" goes back to Manual and automatic messages to that person are blocked. The date and who set it are saved in the activity list.
8. "Send to CRM pipeline" moves it into the CRM. "Mark dead" moves it to Dead.
9. Leads found on Google also show their rating, the two estimates, "Checkup" and "Draft first touch".

### Leads from your website
When someone sends a form on supremeteammedia.com, runs the free business checkup or requests an agreement, they appear here as a New lead with the source "Website" and their message under "What they said". If that person is already a lead (same email or phone), the form is added to their activity list instead of making a second lead. Forms sent on a client's site stay with that client's project and do not appear here. "Send to CRM pipeline" on a website lead uses the inquiry already on file.

### Add a lead
Tap "Add lead". Only the name is required. Source starts as Manual.

### Export
Tap "Export" and choose "CSV spreadsheet" or "PDF call sheet". Both include only the leads the current filters show. The PDF puts one lead per block with room for call notes. Do-not-contact leads are marked: the spreadsheet has a "Do not contact" column and the call sheet prints DO NOT CONTACT in red on that lead.

### Find leads on Google
Open "Find leads on Google", pick a niche and a city, choose how many results, and tap "Mine prospects". Each new business gets a quick checkup and appears in New.

## Good to know

- The dollar estimates come from public data and industry defaults, not their books.
- The activity list at the bottom of each lead shows every logged contact, note and stage change, newest first.
- Switching "Do not contact" off asks you to confirm when the person opted out themselves. Only do it if they told you they want to hear from you again.
- The Dashboard shows the five leads to work today, picked from this list.
