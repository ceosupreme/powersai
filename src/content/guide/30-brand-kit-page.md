# Brand Kit
<!-- part: Running a brand | order: 30 | slug: brand-kit-page | route: /brand-kit | tags: brand kit, brand vault, voice, bio, fonts, do and don't, colors, hex, taglines, hashtags, links, logo, files, assets, content mode, pillars, archive -->

The Brand Kit keeps one brand's identity in one place: how it sounds, its bios, fonts, do and don't rules, colors, taglines, hashtags, links and files, plus how much content HQ makes for it. Open it when you set up a brand, when you need to copy a color code or a bio quickly, or when you want to turn a brand's content production up or down.

## Where to find it
- On a computer, tap "Brand Kit" under "Brand & Assets" in the left menu.
- On a phone, tap "More" in the bottom bar, then "Brand Kit" under "Brand & Assets".
- On a brand's own page, the "Brand kit" link under the brand's name, and the "Open Brand kit" link on its "Setup" tab (and on its "Assets" tab while it has no kit yet), open this screen already set to that brand.
- The kit on screen belongs to the project chosen in the project picker at the top of the screen. With "All projects" chosen, the screen says "Select a project to view its Brand Kit."

## What you see
- The heading "Brand Kit" with the project's name, and "Archive…" on the right ("Restore" when the kit is archived, "Show archived" when there is no active kit).
- A tip box titled "One kit per project", which you can close with its X.
- "Content mode", followed by editable content pillars and mix percentages, with "Save content strategy".
- "Voice & Bios", then "Colors", "Taglines", "Hashtags" and "Links", each with a row at the bottom for adding more.
- "Assets": an upload box that says "Click to upload logos, icons, or photos", then a card per file with a preview for images, its type and size, and download and trash buttons.

## How to use it
### Turn the brand's content production up or down
1. Open the "Content mode" dropdown.
2. Pick "Growth", "Baseline" or "Parked". It saves as soon as you pick; there is no separate save button.

### Write the voice, bios, fonts and rules
1. Fill in any of "Brand Voice", "Short Bio", "Long Bio", "Primary Font", "Secondary Font", "Do" and "Don't".
2. Tap "Save". A "Saved" message confirms it.

### Add colors, taglines, hashtags and links
1. Color: type the hex code (the color code that starts with #) and an optional label such as Primary, then tap "Add".
2. Tagline: type it and an optional "Context", then tap "Add".
3. Hashtag: type it without the # sign and an optional "Group", then tap "Add".
4. Link: type a "Label", the web address and an optional "Category", then tap the plus button.

### Copy something from the kit
1. Tap a color swatch or its code, a tagline, or a hashtag to copy it. Hashtags copy with the # sign in front.
2. For a link, tap its copy button. Tapping the link's name opens it in a new tab instead.
3. A "Copied" message confirms each copy.

### Upload, download or remove files
1. If "Assets" says "Save the brand voice above first to enable uploads.", tap "Save" under "Voice & Bios" first; the kit does not exist yet.
2. Choose "Brand logo" or "Photo or file", then tap the upload box and choose one or more files. An "Uploaded" message appears for each.
3. Use a file's download button to save a copy, or its trash button to remove it.

### Archive, restore or delete the whole kit
1. Tap "Archive…". A box explains the choices and how many records a delete would remove.
2. Add a "Reason (optional)" if you like.
3. Tap "Archive (recommended)" to hide the kit and keep everything, or "Delete permanently" to erase it.
4. To bring an archived kit back, tap "Show archived", then "Restore".

## Words on this screen
- Content mode: how much content HQ produces for the brand. The screen explains it as "Growth gets full weekly production. Baseline runs evergreen work on the clock. Parked gets none."
- Evergreen: content that stays useful for a long time instead of being tied to a date or trend.
- Content pillars: the main topics the brand's content is built around.
- Mix targets: the target share, in percent, for each content purpose, such as teach or entertain. The Production Board compares the brand's last 28 days of pieces with these targets.
- Hex code: the six-character color code that starts with #, for example #1a1a2e.
- Archive: hide the kit without losing anything, so it can be restored later. Each project can have only one kit.

## Good to know
- Enter one content pillar per line. Mix targets must total 100%, or all remain zero when unset. Tap "Save content strategy".
- The X buttons on colors, taglines, hashtags and links delete at once, with no confirmation and no undo.
- A file's trash button deletes the stored file too, even though the tip box says files are kept. Only deleting the whole kit leaves stored files behind.
- Restore an archived kit before editing it. While it is archived and hidden, the add buttons do nothing.
- Foundation checks read the kit’s colors, tagline, social links and logo. Choose "Brand logo" before uploading a logo, or use "Use as logo" on an existing image so the logo check recognizes it.
- The page of each pipeline (a production job in HQ) shows the brand's Short Bio in its "Inputs" card, or its Brand Voice when there is no short bio. The brand's "Assets" tab shows this kit's files and links.
- Some older parts of HQ call this screen the Brand Vault.
