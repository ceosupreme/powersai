# Foundation Audit
<!-- part: Clients and leads | order: 78 | slug: foundation-audit | route: /foundation-audit | tags: foundation audit, money lanes, readiness score, legal, brand, website, google business profile, reviews, social, offers, collateral, gaps, evidence, checklist -->

The Foundation Audit checks whether a business has its basics in place, such as its legal setup, brand, website, Google listing, reviews, social accounts, offers and sales materials. Some items are detected automatically and others you tick yourself, and the result is a readiness score out of 100. Use it to see what must be fixed before money goes into marketing.

## Where to find it

- On a computer, open "Growth & Marketing" in the side menu and choose "Foundation Audit". On a phone, tap "More", then "Foundation Audit".
- When the project chosen in the header is one of your own brands rather than a client, the menu item and the page are called "Money Lanes" instead.
- It shows the project chosen in the header. With "All projects" you are asked to select one.

## What you see

- Three tabs: "Overview", "Categories" and "Gaps". On a phone the tabs show icons only, in that order.
- "Overview": the project with its "Last refresh" date and industry type, a "Refresh audit" button, the "Foundation Readiness" score with counts of items satisfied, missing and not yet evaluated, "Category Scores" tiles and "Recommended next actions".
- A category tile shows its score with "done", "missing" and "partial" counts, or "No data yet" when nothing in it has been answered.
- "Categories": every item, grouped by category. Automatic items show a status icon, a severity, "auto" (or "manual override") and a line such as "Awaiting first detection — run Refresh audit." or the date and signal it was detected from. Manual items have a checkbox, a "manual" badge and an "Evidence URL (optional)" box.
- "Gaps": "All gaps" lists every missing or partial item with its severity, category, status and suggested fix.

## How to use it

### Run the automatic checks
1. Choose the project in the header and open "Overview".
2. Tap "Refresh audit". A message reports how many checks ran, and the automatic items update.

### Tick the manual items
1. Open "Categories".
2. Tick the box when an item is done, such as a business license or insurance. Unticking marks it missing.
3. To keep proof, paste a link into "Evidence URL (optional)". It saves when you leave the box.

### Work the gaps
1. Start with "Recommended next actions" on "Overview", or the full list on "Gaps".
2. Fix the highest-severity items first.
3. Run "Refresh audit" again, or tick the item, to update the score.

## Words on this screen

- Foundation Readiness: the overall score out of 100.
- satisfied, partial, missing, not yet evaluated: an item's state. Partial counts as half.
- auto, manual, manual override: the app detects the item, you tick it yourself, or a manual answer replaced a detected one.
- Severity: "critical", "high", "medium" or "low". Higher severity weighs more in the score.
- Unscored: a category with no answers yet. It is left out of the overall score instead of counting as perfect.
- Evidence URL: a link that proves an item is done, such as a license page or a document.
- Money Lanes: the name this screen uses for your own brands.

## Good to know

- Score colors: 85 and above is green, 70 to 84 is amber, and below 70 is red.
- The checklist comes from the project's industry type, so different industries see different items.
- From this screen, manual items can only be satisfied or missing. "Partial" and "not applicable" cannot be set here, and automatic items cannot be changed by hand.
- "Recommended next actions" shows the top five gaps: missing items first, ranked by severity and by how much their category counts.
- This score is separate from the Growth Score and from the weekly pillar scores.
- The Growth Audit's "Reports" tab includes these results in its reports.
