# Today

Today is your daily starting point. With "All projects" chosen in the header it shows two lists across your active brands: "Next 10", the most valuable steps that are yours to do, and "Waiting on me", the decisions only you can make. With one project chosen it becomes that project's digest of scores, alerts, growth findings and campaigns.

## Where to find it

- Side menu: "Workspace", then "Today". On a phone, "Today" is the first button in the bottom bar. The address is /workspace.
- Signing in opens Portfolio, not Today, so tap "Today" when you want your lists.

## What you see

What appears depends on the project picker in the header.

With "All projects" chosen, the heading "Today" carries the line "Across your active projects · pick a project in the header for its full workspace", and two cards follow:

- "Next 10 across all projects": up to ten rows. Each row has a checkbox, the step, the project name with a short note (such as "P1 · due today", "2 days overdue" or "about $47/hr"), and a tag: "Action", "Lane", "Product" or "KPI". A step that belongs to a pipeline ends with "· Pipeline"; tap it to open that pipeline.
- "Waiting on me", with a count such as "3 to decide". The top three sit on a shaded background. Each row shows the title, the project, "needs your decision", the due date (red when more than a day late), a priority tag "P1" to "P4", and the buttons "Approve", "Reject" and "Snooze". A link such as "Snoozed (2)" opens snoozed items.

With one project chosen, you see:

- The project name with "digest of operations, growth, and marketing", and a red "Critical attention needed" tag when there is a critical growth finding, a campaign whose sync failed, or the readiness gate says "Needs Ops Fix First".
- For client projects only, a "Finish project setup" banner with buttons for missing data sources.
- "Suggested next steps" for this project, when there are any.
- Four tiles. Your brands show "Project score", "Growth score", "Potential" and "Active campaigns". Clients show "Operational score", "Growth score", "Ops Readiness" and "Active campaigns".
- The buttons "Run Audit Now", "Create Campaign" and "Quick Generate".
- "Today's priorities" in three parts, "Operational", "Growth findings" and "Campaigns needing attention", each with "View all".
- "Upcoming opportunities" (dated openings found by the Growth Audit) and links to "Operational scorecard", "Growth Audit" and "Marketing Hub".

## How to use it

### To see your lists across all brands

1. Tap the project picker in the header and choose "All projects".

### To finish a step in Next 10

1. Tick the checkbox beside the step. An "Action" is marked done, a "Lane" is marked as switched on, and a "Product" moves to the stage named in the step.
2. A "KPI" row opens a number box instead. Type this week's number and tap "Save" (or press Enter); "Cancel" closes it.

### To decide on something in Waiting on me

1. Tap "Approve" to accept it. It leaves this list and stays in Next 10 with a small boost.
2. Tap "Reject" to drop it from both lists, or "Snooze" to hide it from both for seven days.
3. To bring a snoozed item back, tap the "Snoozed" link, then "Un-snooze".

### To run quick actions for one project

1. Choose the project in the header.
2. "Run Audit Now" runs that project's Growth Audit; "Audit refresh queued" confirms it started.
3. "Create Campaign" opens the "New campaign" form from Marketing Hub.
4. "Quick Generate" asks for a "Brief" and "Asset kinds" ("Social post" and "GBP post" start selected). Tap "Generate"; the drafts land in the Growth Audit "Action Center", marked "Ad-hoc".

## Words on this screen

- Action: an open step for one of your brands that is not a Claude step, approved or not.
- Lane: a money lane (an earning setup item) not yet switched on and rated critical or high.
- Product: a product at "needs cover", "proof ordered" or "ready to upload".
- KPI: a weekly number with a target, entered by hand, still blank for the current Monday to Sunday week.
- P1 to P4: priority, P1 being the most urgent.
- "needs your decision": not yet approved or rejected.
- "Project score": the weighted average of the brand's pillar scores from its last completed week.
- "Potential": how much of the brand's earning setup (its money lanes) is live, as a percent.
- "Growth score": the Growth Audit score, with an opportunity level such as "High opportunity".

## Good to know

- Only your own brands that are not parked feed the two lists. Clients, prospects, Claude's steps and steps waiting on another unfinished step are left out; steps marked for an agent or a tool still show.
- Next 10 ranks every row on one scale: P1 actions 100, P2 70, P3 45, P4 25, no priority 30, plus 15 when past due, 8 when due within seven days and 5 when approved. Lanes score 40 plus their dollars per hour (up to 95), products 55 to 65, and KPIs 35.
- Next 10 includes steps you have not approved, and ticking one marks it done without approving it.
- If a source cannot be checked, a yellow note names it ("action items", "money lanes", "products" or "KPIs") with "Try again". A red message saying the list "is not trustworthy right now" means the whole check failed.
- The week picker does not change these two lists; they always use the current week.
- The one-project view has no Next 10. A single brand's Next 10 is in its brand workspace.
- "Quick Generate" uses AI. If the AI call fails, it quietly uses built-in sample text: the message ends in "MOCK" instead of "AI", and those drafts are not saved.
- "Operational" alerts and the "Operational scorecard" link (the older Dashboard screen) only have data for clients with point-of-sale feeds.
- Today treats a project as a client only when its type is exactly "client", while Portfolio files every non-brand type under Clients, so a client of another type can show brand-style tiles here.
- The "All brands: waiting on you" strip lives on Portfolio, and Today has no morning brief card.
