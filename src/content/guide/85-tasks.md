# Tasks
<!-- part: Tools and system | order: 85 | slug: tasks | route: /tasks | tags: tasks, to-do, todo, in progress, done, priority, assignee, due date, overdue, badge, batch, comments, next 10, action items, snooze, from insight -->

The Tasks screen is a shared to-do list for every project you can see. Use it to create tasks, assign them, set priorities and due dates, and tick them off. It is a separate list from the action items that feed "Next 10" and "Waiting on me" on Today, and this article explains how the two fit together.

## Where to find it

- On a computer: in the side menu, under "Tools", tap "Tasks".
- On a phone: tap "More" in the bottom bar, then "Tasks" under "Tools".
- From anywhere: tap the magnifying glass in the header (or press Ctrl+K, Command+K on a Mac) and pick "Tasks".

## What you see

- Count boxes for "Total", "Todo", "In Progress", "Done" and "Overdue". They count every task you can see and ignore the filters. "Overdue" gets a red outline when it is above zero.
- A search box, filter menus and a sort menu.
- One card per task: priority, status, title, a "From Insight" tag when the task came from an approved insight, the start of the description, the due date, the time estimate (for example "30m") and the assignee, or "Unassigned". The date turns orange on the due day and reads "Overdue" in red once it has passed. Finished tasks are faded and crossed out.
- Two view buttons next to "New Task": a list and a grid. The grid has three columns, "Todo", "In Progress" and "Done". Cards cannot be dragged between columns.
- The Tasks badge: a red number next to "Tasks" in the side menu. It counts unfinished tasks that are assigned to you or are overdue (anyone's), each counted once, and refreshes about once a minute. The small badge on the icon stops at "9+".

## How to use it

### Create a task
1. Tap "New Task" (on a phone, "New" or the round "+" button at the bottom right).
2. Type the "Title". It is required.
3. Fill in any of "Description", "Priority" (starts at "Medium"), "Assignee", "Due Date" and "Estimate (min)".
4. If you have more than one project, pick one in the field labeled "Bar", an older word for project. It is filled in for you when one project is chosen in the project picker in the header.
5. Tap "Create Task". You see "Task created".

### Change or finish a task
1. Tap the task card. The "Edit Task" panel opens.
2. Tap "Todo", "In Progress" or "Done" at the top. These buttons save right away, and "Done" records the completion date.
3. To change "Title", "Description", "Priority", "Assignee", "Due Date" or "Time Estimate (min)", edit them and tap "Save Changes".
4. Scroll down for "Comments" and the "Activity" history.

### Comment on a task
1. Open the task and type in "Write a comment... Use @ to mention someone".
2. To tag someone, type @ and pick the name. The name is highlighted, but nobody is notified.
3. Tap the send button (or press Ctrl+Enter, Command+Enter on a Mac).
4. Your own comments have a menu with "Edit" and "Delete".

### Find tasks
1. Type in "Search tasks...". It searches titles only.
2. Narrow the list with the menus for status, priority, due date ("Overdue", "Due Today", "This Week"), assignee (including "Unassigned") and project. The project menu only appears when you have more than one project.
3. Change the order with the sort menu: "Due Date ↑" (the default), "Due Date ↓", "Priority ↑", "Priority ↓", "Newest", "Oldest" or "Title A-Z".
4. Tap "Clear" (an X on a phone) to reset the filters.

### Update many tasks at once
1. Tap "Select All". A checkbox appears on every task in the list.
2. Untick the ones you want to leave alone.
3. In the bar at the bottom, tap "Done", "In Progress" or "Delete", or tap "Cancel" to stop.

### Delete a task
1. Open the task and tap the red trash button next to "Save Changes".
2. Tap "Delete" to confirm. There is no undo and no archive for tasks.

### Snooze something for later
Tasks cannot be snoozed. Snoozing belongs to action items on Today.
1. Open "Today" and find the item under "Waiting on me".
2. Tap "Snooze". It leaves "Waiting on me" and "Next 10" for seven days, and you see "Snoozed for a week".
3. To bring it back early, tap "Snoozed" at the bottom of "Waiting on me", then "Un-snooze".

## Words on this screen

- Task: an item on this list. It belongs to one project and has a status and a priority.
- Action item: a separate kind of to-do that feeds "Next 10" and "Waiting on me". HQ's crews, insights and the "New Task" button in the header create them.
- Next 10: the ranked list of next steps on Today and on each project's home page. It is built from action items, money lanes, products and weekly numbers, never from this list.
- From Insight: a task made when you approved an insight's suggested action.
- Priority: "Critical", "High", "Medium" or "Low". Action items use P1 to P4 instead.
- Status: "Todo", "In Progress" or "Done".
- Overdue: the due date is before today and the task is not done.
- This Week: due between today and seven days from now.
- Activity: an automatic record of status, priority and assignee changes.

## Good to know

- The two lists are not linked. Finishing a task here does not tick off an action item in "Next 10", and finishing an action item does not touch this list.
- Tasks arrive here four ways: "New Task" on this screen; approving an insight's suggested action, which also marks the action item approved, so the same work then shows in both places; routing a Capture Inbox item as a task; and adding a linked task inside a Content Pipeline item.
- The "New Task" button in the header (a plain "+" on a phone) does not add to this screen. It creates an approved action item for "Next 10" and sends it to Asana. The task buttons on Weekly Review and on insight cards open that same form.
- The "New Task" quick action in search opens this screen but not the create form.
- Assignee menus also list a few names from an older Asana team who have no HQ login. Choosing one makes the save fail, and filtering by one shows "Failed to load tasks. Please try again."
- As the admin you see every task. Anyone else sees only tasks for the projects assigned to them.
- Batch "Delete" removes tasks at once, with no confirmation.
- Filters reset when you leave the screen.
- The owner's phone menu shows no Tasks badge. Managers and staff see it on their bottom bar.
