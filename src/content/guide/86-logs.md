# Logs
<!-- part: Tools and system | order: 86 | slug: logs | route: /logs | tags: logs, daily log, incident, accident, maintenance, guest issue, shift notes, shoutout, voice note, 86 update, report, form, draft, submitted -->

Logs is where you file a written record for one project: an incident, a maintenance problem, shift notes, a shoutout, a full daily log or a quick voice note. Use it when something happened that should be on record, and open "Previous Logs" to read what has already been filed. Several log types use restaurant language, such as "86 Update", but they work for any project.

## Where to find it

- On a computer: in the side menu, under "Tools", tap "Logs".
- On a phone: tap "More" in the bottom bar, then "Logs" under "Tools".
- From search: tap the magnifying glass and pick "Logs" or the quick action "New Log".
- Related addresses: /logs/new and /logs/:id/edit both bring you back to this screen, /logs/:id opens a single log, and /logs/interview/:id is switched off and also brings you back here.
- Shift leads and staff have their own logs screen, described in "Roles, role preview and staff screens".

## What you see

- The heading "Logs" and the line "Report incidents, capture notes, or complete your daily log".
- If no single project is chosen in the project picker, the screen says "No Project Selected" and "Please select a project to access logs."
- A grid of cards: "Incident", "Accident", "Maintenance", "Guest Issue", "Shift Notes", "Shoutout", "Voice Note" and "Daily Log". Leads and Contributor staff also get "86 Update". Your role decides which cards show.
- A "Previous Logs" bar with the number of entries. It opens the submitted logs for the chosen project, newest first, each with its type, a green "Submitted" badge, the date and time, and who filed it.

## How to use it

### File a log
1. Choose a single project in the project picker in the header.
2. Tap a card. "Daily Log" opens the "GM Daily Log" form for you ("Shift Lead Daily Log" for leads). Every other card except "Voice Note" opens the same "Staff Quick Log" form.
3. Fill in the fields. The "Progress" bar counts the required ones, for example "2/5 required". Some fields only appear after you answer an earlier one, and fields with a microphone button let you dictate.
4. Tap "Submit Log" (pinned to the bottom on a phone). If something required is empty, you see "Please fill in:" with the field names.
5. When you see "Log Submitted!", tap "Log Another" or "Done". "Back" at the top returns to the cards without saving.

### Record a voice note
1. Tap "Voice Note".
2. Tap the red microphone ("Tap to start recording"), allow the microphone if asked, speak, then tap again to stop.
3. Wait while it shows "Transcribing...", then read the transcript.
4. Tap "Save Note" to keep it or "Discard" to try again.

### Read a past log
1. Tap "Previous Logs", then tap a log.
2. The log page shows the type, the status, who filed it, when it was created and when it was submitted, then one card per section with each answer. Empty answers show a dash, yes or no questions show "Yes" or "No", and ratings show like "7/10".
3. Tap "Back to Logs" to return.

### Delete a draft
Only unfinished drafts you created yourself can be deleted. Logs filed on this screen are submitted straight away, so drafts come from the staff logs screen.
1. Open the draft.
2. Tap "Delete Draft", then "Delete". This cannot be undone.

## Words on this screen

- Log: a filled-in form saved for one project.
- Staff Quick Log, GM Daily Log and Shift Lead Daily Log: the three log forms. Which one opens depends on the card and your role.
- Draft: an unfinished log. Only its author can delete it.
- Submitted: a finished log. It cannot be edited or deleted.
- Required: a field that must be filled in before the log can be submitted.
- 86 Update: a restaurant term for marking menu items as unavailable.
- Voice note: a short recording that HQ turns into text.

## Good to know

- The card you tap is not saved with the log. An incident, a shoutout and shift notes are all stored as a "Staff Quick Log", so the list cannot tell them apart. Put the kind of report in the first answer.
- There is no edit screen. The edit address sends you back to Logs, so check your answers before you submit.
- Saved voice notes do not appear in "Previous Logs" or anywhere else in HQ. The only way to see them is the "Voice Notes" export in Admin Panel, "Settings", "Backup & Export".
- The questions on each form come from HQ's form setup. There is no screen in HQ for changing them.
- Voice Interview mode is switched off, so its address always returns to Logs.
- If the microphone is blocked you see "Could not access microphone", and a very short clip shows "Recording too short".
- The page switch for this screen is called "Daily Logs" in Admin Panel, "Permissions".
