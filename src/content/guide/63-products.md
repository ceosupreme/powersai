# Products

Products, headed "Products & Courses", is one shared catalog of everything you sell or plan to sell, with where each one is listed and which projects promote it. Use it to track a product from idea to live, to record its outlets, and to tie content and revenue back to it.

## Where to find it
- On a computer, tap "Products" under "Brand & Assets" in the left menu.
- On a phone, tap "More" in the bottom bar, then "Products" under "Brand & Assets".
- On a brand's page, "View all" on the "Products by stage" section opens this list already filtered to that brand.
- The main brand selector filters the list by saved brand links. All brands shows the whole accessible catalog.

## What you see
- The heading "Products & Courses", the line "Shared library; tag channels that promote each product", and a "New Product" button.
- A tip box titled "Account-wide library". You can close it with its X.
- Three filters: "All brands", "All statuses" and "All outlets".
- A table with "Name", "Brands", "Outlets", "Price", "Stage", "Status", "Monthly Sales", "Sales Page" (an "Open" link), and edit and delete buttons. In "Outlets", each outlet is a small label, filled in when the product is listed there and outlined otherwise. On a phone, swipe the table sideways.
- With nothing to show, the table says "No products yet."

## How to use it
### Add a product
1. Tap "New Product".
2. Type the "Name". It is the only required field.
3. Fill in "Price (USD)", "Funnel Stage" and "Status". New products start at the core stage with the status idea.
4. Add a "Lead Magnet", "Monthly Sales (manual)", "Sales Page URL" and "Notes" if you have them.
5. Under "Channels promoting this product", tick the projects that promote it.
6. Tap "Add".

### Record where the product is sold
1. Save the product first, then reopen it with the pencil button. Until then the form says "Save the product first, then reopen it to add outlet listings."
2. In the bottom row under "Outlets", pick the outlet and a status (planned, listed, paused or removed), add the web address, price, listed date and notes, and tap "Add outlet".
3. To change a listing, edit it in place. The status and date save as soon as you change them; the web address, price and notes save when you leave the box.
4. Tap a listing's trash button to remove it.

### Filter the list
1. Use "All brands", "All statuses" and "All outlets" to narrow the table.
2. The page's Brand filter and the main selector stay in sync. Existing links containing a project selection still open that brand.

### Delete a product
1. Tap the trash button on the product's row.
2. Confirm. Linked content items and revenue entries keep their data but lose the link to the product.


### Search and sort products
1. Search by product name or notes.
2. Narrow the results with the labeled Brand, Status and Outlet filters.
3. Choose "Sort by" for name, newest, oldest or highest price. Products without a price sort last.

## Words on this screen
- Product: anything you sell, such as a book, course, printable or template.
- Brands: the projects linked as promoting the product.
- Outlet: a place the product is listed for sale, such as Etsy, Amazon KDP (Amazon's self-publishing service for books), Kindle, Gumroad, Payhip, TikTok Shop or Own store.
- Listing status: planned, listed, paused or removed, for one product at one outlet.
- Funnel Stage: the product's role in your sales path: lead magnet (a free item that collects contacts), tripwire (a small first purchase), core (the main offer), upsell (an add-on after a purchase) or continuity (a repeat subscription or membership).
- Status: how far along the product is, from idea through drafting and ready to upload to live, or cut.
- Monthly Sales (manual): a number you type in yourself. HQ does not count sales.
- Sales Page: the web page where people buy the product.

## Good to know
- The project-link picker includes every accessible project type. Existing links are loaded before Save becomes available, so editing a product preserves its brand and project links.
- Each product can have one listing per outlet. Adding the same outlet again replaces that listing's details.
- Outlet listings save on their own as you edit them; tapping "Cancel" on the form does not undo them.
- HQ's weekly numbers run counts only listings marked listed, on products linked to a brand, for that brand's products live, listings live and KDP titles numbers. It does not check the marketplace itself.
- Content items and revenue entries can point at a product through their "Linked Product" field, and a product's pipeline page lists the revenue entries linked to it.

## Main brand selection
Products follows the main brand or client selector using its saved brand links. Choose All brands to see the entire accessible catalog, including products with no links. One product can belong to more than one brand. Changing the view does not change its links.
