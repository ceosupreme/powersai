# Setup tab
<!-- part: Running a brand | order: 22 | slug: setup-tab | route: /project/:venueId | tags: setup, setup map, setup score, accounts, assets, to set up, in place, needs attention, i already have it, later, skip, set it up, top 3, setup brief, brand type, ready to grow, caution, set up first -->

The "Accounts to set up" tab is the map of every account and asset this brand should have, such as its website, social accounts, stores and money accounts, and which of them are already in place. It gives the brand a setup score, says why each item matters and roughly how much of your time it takes, and starts setup pipelines for the gaps. Use it when you start a brand, when its setup score is low, or when you want a ready-made list of accounts to create.

## Where to find it

- Open the brand, then tap "Accounts to set up" in the tab row.
- Or tap "Open Setup" in the setup line at the top of "Overview".
- Or tap "Open the Setup tab" in a traffic check notice on a pipeline.
- The address ends in ?tab=setup.

## What you see

The header:
- A headline such as "Setup: 6 of 14 in place, 8 to set up".
- The score and its gate word, for example "Setup score 64, Caution", with this note under it: "Each item counts by how much it matters; an account that needs access counts half; skipped items don't count. It is 20 percent of the brand's overall score."
- "Brand type:" followed by one badge for each kind that fits the brand, or "Not set", and an "Edit" button.
- Two buttons: "Set up the top 3" and "Copy setup brief".

Then five sections, each with its count in brackets. A section with nothing in it says "None."
- "To set up": items this brand needs that do not exist yet. They are grouped under "Foundation", "Home base", "Social", "Video and audio", "Stores and marketplaces", "Money accounts", "Local" and "Software". Each row shows:
  - The item's name and a "To set up" badge.
  - A "Why:" line built from up to three reasons: "Standard for this kind of brand", "Fits" followed by the brand's niches, and "Needed for" followed by money avenues.
  - What the item unlocks for the brand.
  - A time line: "About 15 minutes of your time", "Claude makes this" (it takes none of your time) or "Time not estimated". A "Playbook" link follows when a playbook covers the item.
  - Buttons: "Set it up" (or "Open setup job" when a setup pipeline is already running), "I already have it", "Later" and "Skip". Foundation rows have no "I already have it".
- "Needs attention": accounts that exist but you cannot get into ("Exists, needs access"), and accounts that are planned but not created ("Planned, not set up"). Each row shows its group and handle, with "Set it up" or "Open setup job". Planned rows also have "I already have it".
- "In place": items that are done, grouped the same way. Accounts show their status ("Live", "Dormant", "Empty", "Planned" or "Needs access"), the handle with an "Open" link to the account, and a follower line such as "1,240 followers on 2026-10-30" or "No follower count yet". Foundation items say "On the Brand kit page." with an "Open Brand kit" link.
- "Later and Skipped": folded shut; tap it to open. Items you put off or ruled out, each with its reason and an "Undo" button.
- "More options": folded shut. Items that are optional for this brand, each with what it unlocks and an "Add to this brand's plan" button.

Only admins see the buttons. Everyone else sees the map without them.

## How to use it

### To set the brand type
1. Tap "Edit" next to "Brand type:".
2. Tick every kind that fits: "Creator (personal brand)", "Lifestyle creator", "Content brand", "YouTube-first channel", "Local news", "Music artist", "Music label", "Agency", "App" or "Merch brand".
3. Tap "Save". HQ shows "Brand type saved" and the map updates to match the new type.

### To start setup for one item
1. In "To set up" or "Needs attention", find the item and tap "Set it up".
2. For a Foundation item, HQ opens the Brand Kit page for this brand. Fill it in there.
3. For any other item, HQ creates a setup pipeline and immediately opens that plan. If the brand had no record of the account, HQ marks one as planned.
4. On return to Accounts to set up, the row provides an "Open setup job" link so you can resume.

### To start the three most important items at once
1. Tap "Set up the top 3".
2. Read the list and the total, for example "About 40 minutes of your time in total."
3. Tap "Start all 3". HQ creates the plans and opens the first one. The others remain available in the plans list; partial failures are reported. Tap "Cancel" to back out.

### To record an account you already have
1. Tap "I already have it" on the row.
2. If the item can live on more than one platform, choose the "Platform".
3. Fill in "Handle" or "Link". At least one is needed. Add "Followers (optional)" if you know the number.
4. Choose a "Status": "Live" or "Dormant".
5. Tap "Save". HQ shows that the item was recorded, and the map counts it. Any earlier Later or Skip choice for it is cleared.

### To put an item off
1. Tap "Later" on the row. HQ shows that it moved to Later, and it drops into "Later and Skipped".

### To rule an item out
1. Tap "Skip" on the row.
2. Type the "Reason". The reason stays with the brand.
3. Tap "Skip it".

### To undo Later or Skip
1. Tap "Later and Skipped" to open it.
2. Tap "Undo" on the item. HQ shows "Decision undone" and the item returns to the map.

### To add an optional item to the plan
1. Tap "More options" to open it.
2. Tap "Add to this brand's plan" on the item. HQ shows that it was added, and it now counts as needed.

### To copy a setup brief
1. Tap "Copy setup brief". HQ shows "Setup brief copied".
2. Paste it into a note or a chat. It lists the brand and niche; the colors, fonts and voice from the brand kit; the handles already in use; and each gap with what it is, why it is needed, the time it takes, a link to its playbook and plain steps. It ends with "Never paste passwords into chat or HQ; log in directly on the platform."

## Words on this screen

- Asset: anything a brand needs set up, such as a social account, a store, a money account or its brand kit.
- "To set up": needed, and not created yet.
- "Exists, needs access": the account exists but you cannot get into it yet. It counts half toward the score.
- "Planned, not set up": HQ has a record for the account but it has not been created yet.
- "In place": done and recorded.
- "Later": put off for now, without a reason.
- "Skipped": ruled out for this brand, with a reason. Skipped items do not count toward the score.
- "Optional": not needed for this kind of brand, but available to add.
- "Foundation": the brand kit itself. It is filled in on the Brand Kit page, never through a setup pipeline.
- "Setup score": the share of the brand's needed items that are in place, where more important items count more.
- "Ready to grow": a setup score of 70 or more.
- "Caution": a setup score of 50 to 69.
- "Set up first": a setup score under 50.
- Setup pipeline: a production job that walks through setting up one account, step by step.
- Brand type: the kinds of brand this is. It decides which items the map treats as standard.

## Good to know

- The score on this tab is read fresh from the database each time. The copy used by Portfolio and by the brand's overall score refreshes nightly, so a change here can take until the next morning to show there.
- Items in "Later" still count against the score as not done. Only "Skip" takes an item out of the score, so skip only what this brand will truly never need.
- "Set up the top 3" picks the three highest-priority items under "To set up" that are not Foundation items and have no open setup pipeline. The button is grey when there is nothing to pick.
- A setup pipeline started here has five fixed steps: create or confirm the account and handle (you), profile copy, link and brand mark from the kit (Claude), apply the profile and connect the scheduler (you), the first five pieces at the channel's level (Claude), and a cadence check in 14 days (Claude). A media kit item gets three steps instead: Claude drafts the media kit and rate card, you check the rates and approve, and Claude saves the final link on the brand's channels.
- Pipelines started here are created at Level 1 and skip the "Create pipeline" form. A setup started from the "Channels" tab opens the form, where you can pick the level and niche.
- Nothing on this tab creates an account on any platform. You create or claim the account yourself, and HQ records it.
- Never paste passwords into HQ or into a chat. Log in directly on the platform.
