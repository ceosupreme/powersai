# Channel Revenue
<!-- part: Money and offers | order: 60 | slug: channel-revenue | route: /revenue | tags: revenue, income, money, earnings, channel revenue, monthly totals, ad revenue, affiliate, course, sponsor, merch, coaching, payout -->

Channel Revenue is where you log the money a project earned, one entry per kind of income per month. Add an entry whenever a payout lands, so the month's totals stay right here, on the brand's page and on Portfolio.

## Where to find it
- On a computer, tap "Channel Revenue" under "Growth & Marketing" in the left menu.
- On a phone, tap "More" in the bottom bar, then "Channel Revenue" under "Growth & Marketing".
- On a brand's page, "View all" on the "Revenue this month" section opens this screen for that brand.
- The screen shows the project chosen in the project picker at the top of the screen. If the picker is on "All projects", it says "Select a channel to view its revenue."

## What you see
- The heading "Channel Revenue" with the project's name, and a "New Entry" button.
- A tip box titled "Channel Revenue". You can close it with its X.
- Three summary cards: this month's total, marked "(This Month)"; last month's total, marked "(Last Month)"; and "By Type", which splits this month's total by kind of income.
- A table of entries, newest month first, with "Month", "Type", "Amount", "Note", and edit and delete buttons. On a phone, swipe it sideways.
- With no entries yet, the table says "No revenue entries yet. Add your first entry to start tracking."

## How to use it
### Log income
1. Check that the project picker shows the right project.
2. Tap "New Entry".
3. Pick the "Type": "Ad Revenue", "Affiliate", "Course", "Sponsor", "Merch" or "Coaching".
4. Type the "Amount (USD)" and pick the "Month". The month starts on the current one.
5. Add a "Source Note", such as the payout or sponsor name, and pick a "Linked Product" if the money came from one product.
6. Tap "Add". A "Revenue entry added" message confirms it.

### Fix or remove an entry
1. Tap the pencil on the entry's row, change what you need, and tap "Save".
2. To remove it, tap the trash button and confirm "Delete this revenue entry?".

## Words on this screen
- Entry: one amount of income, for one type, in one month.
- Type: the kind of income. The choices are fixed: Ad Revenue, Affiliate, Course, Sponsor, Merch and Coaching.
- Source Note: your own note on where the money came from.
- Linked Product: the product from the Products screen that earned this money.
- This Month and Last Month: totals for the current and previous calendar month, by your device's date.

## Good to know
- These entries show up elsewhere: each brand card on Portfolio shows this month's total, Portfolio has a "Revenue this month by project" list and a "Money this month" sort, a brand's page shows "Revenue this month", and a product's pipeline page lists recent entries linked to that product.
- The tip box says these entries feed the Monetization pillar in the Weekly Review. Not confirmed: no code was found that grades a pillar from these entries. Revenue numbers on brand scorecards come from separate measurements, such as sales read from a connected store, not from this screen.
- Entries only come from what you log. HQ's weekly numbers run, which reads store sales for brand scores, is set up never to write entries here, so log store income yourself if you want it in these totals.
- You can log several entries for the same type and month; the totals add them together.
- Income that doesn't fit the six types, such as client work or book royalties, has no type of its own. Pick the closest type and say what it is in the "Source Note".
- Amounts are in US dollars, and each entry counts toward exactly one month.
