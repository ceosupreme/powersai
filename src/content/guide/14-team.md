# Team
<!-- part: Workspace | order: 14 | slug: team | route: /employees | tags: team, employees, staff, people, compliance, violations, overtime, meal break, no clockout, premium pay, exposure, tenure, new hires, all-stars, follow up, hours, wins, concerns, toast -->

Team lists the hourly staff of the selected client project, pulled from that client's point-of-sale time clock, with their hours, tenure and labor compliance alerts. Use it to see who needs a follow-up conversation and how much premium pay a client may owe for missed meal breaks. It is not a list of HQ users, and your own brands have no staff here.

## Where to find it

- Side menu: "Workspace", then "Team". On a phone, tap "More", then "Team". The address is /employees.
- Tap a person to open their page (the address is /employees/ followed by the person's code). A team member's name on an insight card also opens this page.
- Team shows the project chosen in the header picker.

## What you see

On the list page:

- The heading "Team" with the project name and a count such as "18 of 24".
- Five tiles: "Active employees", "Compliance · 30d" (with "more" or "fewer vs. prior 30d"), "Premium pay exposure", "Avg tenure" and "New hires · 30d".
- Three chips: "All-stars", "Follow up" and "New hires".
- "Search by name…", a status menu ("Active", "Inactive", "All statuses") and a role menu ("All roles" plus each role found).
- On a computer, a table with "Name", "Role", "Status", "Tenure", "Hours 90d", "Violations (Week)", "Violations 90d", "Wins 90d" and "Concerns 90d". On a phone, one card per person with the same numbers.

On a person's page:

- "All employees" to go back, then the name, role, an "Active" or "Inactive" tag and days of tenure.
- "Project context", when the person works at more than one project, to switch which project's records you see.

The person's page has four tabs:

- "Overview": "Tenure", "Hours · last 90d" with a small chart and weekly average, "Violations · last 90d" with a breakdown (such as "Late meal break: 2"), and "Most recent shift".
- "Compliance": a six-month trend chart, "Overtime tracking" ("OT this week", "OT trailing 4 weeks", "Days > 8h (4w)", "Weeks > 40h (4w)"), "Estimated premium pay exposure", and each alert with its severity and status.
- "Performance": "Tenure", "Compliance · last 90d" and "Reliability · weekly hours".
- "Activity": a timeline of shifts, compliance alerts, wins and concerns, with "Load more shifts" for older history.

## How to use it

### To see a client's staff

1. In the header, choose the client in the project picker.
2. Open "Team". The list starts with active staff only.

### To find people who need attention

1. Tap the "Follow up" chip, or the "Compliance · 30d" tile, which turns on the same chip.
2. Tap a person to read their "Compliance" tab.
3. Tap the chip or tile again to clear it.

### To filter and sort the list

1. Type part of a name in "Search by name…", or pick a status or role.
2. On a computer, tap a column heading such as "Hours 90d" to sort; tap it again to flip the order. "Role" and "Status" do not sort.

### To look at one person

1. Tap their row or card.
2. Switch tabs with "Overview", "Compliance", "Performance" and "Activity". On "Activity", tap "Load more shifts" to go further back 90 days at a time.

## Words on this screen

- Violation: a labor compliance alert, such as a late meal break, a missed meal break, overtime, a multi-location shift or a missing clock-out.
- "Violations (Week)": alerts in the last completed Monday to Sunday week, Pacific time.
- "Compliance · 30d": how many people had at least one alert in the last 30 days, compared with the 30 days before.
- "Premium pay exposure": one hour of pay per missed meal break, the California rule; late meals do not count. "Alerts missing wage" means a wage is not on file, so the amount is too low.
- Tenure: days since the hire date, or since the first recorded shift when no hire date is on file.
- "All-stars": 90 or more days of tenure, no violations in 90 days, hours worked, and steady weekly hours.
- "Follow up": three or more violations in 90 days, or two or more missing clock-outs in 30 days.
- "New hires": fewer than 30 days of tenure.
- Wins and Concerns: positive and negative insights about the person in the last 90 days.
- Reliability: how much weekly hours swing: "Consistent", "Variable", "Highly variable" or "Insufficient data" (fewer than three weeks).

## Good to know

- Team was built for bar and restaurant clients whose time clock feeds HQ. For your own brands, or with "All projects" chosen, the list is empty and says "No employees match the current filters."
- "Active employees", "Compliance · 30d" and "New hires · 30d" can be tapped to filter the list; "Premium pay exposure" and "Avg tenure" cannot.
- Without a hire date, tenure counts only from the first shift in the last 90 days, so it can read lower than it really is.
- If "All-stars" finds nobody, a note explains how many days of tenure are needed and the longest tenure on record.
- The tab you open is kept in the page address, so a shared link opens on the same tab.

When a brand is selected, HQ routes legacy venue reports to the brand workspace. The Team entry directs brand work to Tasks and Chat; venue staffing reports remain available for client locations.
