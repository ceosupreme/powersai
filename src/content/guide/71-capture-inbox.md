# Capture Inbox
<!-- part: Clients and leads | order: 71 | slug: capture-inbox | route: /inbox | tags: capture inbox, inbox, quick capture, ideas, notes, tasks, route, routing, archive, email, hello mailbox, forwarded -->

The Capture Inbox is a holding place for quick thoughts: ideas, tasks, notes and possible leads. You drop them in from any screen in a few seconds and sort them later, when you have time. Its "Email" tab also shows mail sent to the company's hello mailbox.

## Where to find it

- On a phone, tap "Inbox" in the bottom bar.
- On a computer, open the "Clients & Leads" group in the side menu and choose "Capture Inbox". The page is titled "Capture Inbox".
- To capture something without leaving the screen you are on, tap the inbox icon at the top right of any HQ screen.

## What you see

- Four tabs: "Inbox" (items waiting to be sorted), "Routed" (items you sorted, with the type you chose), "Archived" and "Email".
- A help tip, "AI suggests, you accept", which you can close with its X.
- Each waiting item shows its text, the time, a "Project" menu, a "Save as" menu, a "Route item" button and an archive button.
- When AI suggestions are switched on, an item can also show a line such as "Suggest: task · (project)" with an "Accept" button, or a "Suggest routing" button.
- The "Email" tab lists up to 100 recent emails. Each shows the sender, a "New" badge until it is read, "Forwarded" or "Not forwarded", the subject, a short preview, the time, "Reply" and "Mark read".

## How to use it

### Capture something fast
1. Tap the inbox icon at the top right of any screen.
2. Type your thought in the box.
3. Tap "Save". A "Captured" message appears with an "Inbox" shortcut.
4. On a computer, Ctrl+Shift+K (Cmd+Shift+K on a Mac) opens the box, and Ctrl+Enter (Cmd+Enter) saves it.

### Sort an item
1. Open the "Inbox" tab.
2. Choose a "Project" and a "Save as" destination.
3. Tap "Route item". HQ creates the destination and moves the capture to "Routed" together. If either fails, the capture stays in the Inbox. Repeating a completed request does not duplicate its destination.
4. If a suggestion line shows, you can tap "Accept" instead to route it the suggested way.

### Archive an item
1. Tap the archive button (the small box icon) at the end of the item's row.
2. The item moves to "Archived".

### Read and answer email
1. Open the "Email" tab.
2. Tap "Reply" to open your email app with the subject already filled in.
3. Tap "Mark read" to clear the "New" badge.


### Find captures to sort
1. Use "Search" to find text in the selected capture tab.
2. Choose "Sort by" for oldest first, newest first or alphabetical order. The default is oldest first.
3. Choose a labeled "Project" and "Save as" destination, then select "Route item". A project is required except for a CRM lead.
4. Destination names use ordinary words, such as "Content idea" and "Brand asset".

## Words on this screen

- Capture: saving a raw note to the inbox without sorting it.
- Route: giving an item a type, and usually a project, so it leaves the inbox.
- task: creates a task in the chosen project, using the first line as the title and the full text as the description. A project is required.
- crm_lead: creates a company in the CRM named after the first line, with the status "prospect". No contact is added.
- idea and content_idea create an idea-stage content item in the selected project. note creates a draft knowledge-base entry. brand_asset adds a link to the project’s Brand Kit and requires a single http(s) URL; upload local files directly in Brand Kit.
- Forwarded: a copy of the email was sent on to your personal inbox.

## Good to know

- Saving a capture is instant and does not use AI.
- AI suggestions depend on a back-end switch. When disabled, HQ shows that suggestions are off; you can still choose a project and type yourself.
- Routed and archived items cannot be moved back or routed a second time.
- The "Inbox" button on the phone bar opens this page, not new website leads. New leads are in "CRM", on the "Inbound" tab.
- Emails sent to the hello mailbox are saved, matched to a lead with the same address and forwarded to you. When a lead replies this way, any follow-up drafts still waiting for that lead are canceled.
- Captures belong to your whole account; the project picker in the header does not filter them.
