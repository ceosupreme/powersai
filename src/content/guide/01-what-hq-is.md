# What HQ is and how it fits together
<!-- part: Start here | order: 1 | slug: what-hq-is | route: none | tags: overview, start, brands, projects, how it works, big picture -->

Supreme Team HQ is the back office that runs every brand, channel, app and client project you have, all in one place. This article explains the big pieces and how they connect, so every other article in this guide makes sense.

## The big idea

Every brand you own is set up in HQ the same way. HQ knows what accounts the brand has and what it is still missing, it holds the step-by-step method for every kind of thing you make, it turns ideas into finished pieces through pipelines, it keeps the numbers, and it scores each brand so you always know where to spend your time.

Claude works inside HQ while you sleep. It does the research, drafting and filing steps every night and leaves you only the steps that need you: decisions, approvals, logins and publishing.

## What HQ holds

- Your brands. Supreme Team Media, Allmighty Supreme, Big Paws Club, Supreme Wellness, Supreme Survival, Coastal Beauties, Jenna Banks, Napa Valley Vibes, DJ Redlite and the rest are set up as internal brands.
- Your channel projects. San Diego Remix, Temecula & Murrieta News, Divine Duality, Supreme Gaming and the other channel brands are set up as content channels.
- A holding project called "Unassigned pages and channels" for pages and accounts that do not belong to a brand yet.
- Your app. BarPulse is set up as an app build.
- Client and prospect businesses. Bars, restaurants, plumbers, home service companies and auto shops that came in through audits, the free check and proposals.

HQ calls each of these a project. Most screens have a project picker at the top, and the brand workspace opens one project at a time.

## How the pieces connect

1. The setup map lists every account and asset a brand should have, compares it with what is on file, and gives the brand a Setup score.
2. Playbooks hold the method for every format, platform, money path and niche. Your own material goes into them first.
3. Radar finds what to make next. Each month it researches every channel brand and files ready-to-make briefs.
4. When you tap to make a brief, HQ creates a production job, also called a pipeline. Each step belongs to Claude or to you.
5. Claude does its steps overnight, saves the files to the brand's Drive folder and links them on the step. Your steps show up on Today in "Next 10 across all projects", and in the brand's "Waiting on you" count on its Overview tab.
6. Numbers come in from screenshots, Metricool, and what you enter by hand. They feed each brand's pillar scores.
7. Portfolio rolls everything up: one card per brand with its overall score, setup progress and money.

## Who does what

- You make the decisions, approve work, log in to platforms, publish, and do any step marked as yours.
- Claude runs the scheduled crews: research, scripts, drafts, files, playbooks, and a report email after each run. The production crew never publishes, never spends money and never marks your steps done. The article "The crews that run while you sleep" lists every crew and its limits.
- An assistant or a bot can follow a brand's setup brief and the playbooks to finish setup work for you. Staff accounts only see the screens their role allows.
- Lovable is the builder behind HQ's screens. Small screen fixes are made directly in the code at no cost. Bigger builds go through Lovable, which costs credits, so Claude always asks you first and tells you the rough cost.

## The three questions HQ answers

- What needs me today? Open "Today".
- How is each brand doing? Open "Portfolio".
- What is still missing? Open a brand and look at its "Setup" tab.

## How this guide is organized

The guide is split into parts that follow the menu: Start here, Workspace, Running a brand, Making things, Research and playbooks, Money and offers, Clients and leads, Tools and system, and Reference. Most articles have the same sections: where to find the screen, what you see, how to use it step by step, the words on the screen, and things worth knowing.

Use the search box at the top of the Guide to find any word. The "Open this screen" button on an article takes you straight to the screen it describes. The same text is saved as a PDF for your records, and "Print the whole guide" prints every article in order.

## Good to know

- HQ lives at supremeteammedia.com. The public website and the back office are the same site; the back office needs your login.
- Brands and clients share the same building blocks, but brands are scored on growth, money and setup, while clients are scored on revenue, team, delivery and client experience.
- When a screen and this guide disagree, the screen is right and the guide needs an update. Tell Claude which screen and it will fix the matching article.
