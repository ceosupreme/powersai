# Products tab
<!-- part: Running a brand | order: 25 | slug: products-tab | route: /project/:venueId | tags: products, start production, pipeline, listings, outlets, price, sales per month, funnel stage, product status, product type -->

The "Products" tab shows the products linked to this brand, such as books, printables and templates, with their status, price, monthly sales and where they are listed. From each product you can start a production pipeline, or open the one already running. Use it to see the brand's catalog at a glance and to start making a product.

## Where to find it

- Open the brand, then tap "Products" in the tab row.
- The address ends in ?tab=products.

## What you see

- One card per product, with an orange top edge. Cards are sorted by status, earliest stage first, then by name.
  - The product's name and a status badge: "Idea", "Scored", "Brief", "Drafting", "Needs Cover", "Proof Ordered", "Ready To Upload", "Live", "Twin Listed" or "Cut".
  - A line with the funnel stage ("Lead Magnet", "Tripwire", "Core", "Upsell" or "Continuity", or "No Stage"), the price (or "No Price") and sales per month (such as "12 Sales/mo", or "— Sales/mo" when unknown).
  - One small badge per listing, showing the outlet and the listing's status, such as "Etsy: listed". With none, it says "No listings yet".
  - A "Start production" button, or a "Pipeline" button when a pipeline already exists for the product.
- With nothing linked, the tab says "No products linked to this brand."

## How to use it

### To start making a product
1. Tap "Start production" on its card.
2. The "Create pipeline" form opens already filled in. The title is the product's name, the product type is guessed from the name, the outlets it is listed on are ticked as destinations, and direct sale (monetization) and video to product (promotion) are ticked when those playbooks exist.
3. Check the "Product type", which is required, and the other choices.
4. Tap "Create pipeline". The new pipeline page opens, and the card's button changes to "Pipeline".

### To open a product's pipeline
1. Tap "Pipeline" on its card.

## Words on this screen

- Product: something the brand sells, such as a printable, a planner or a book.
- Funnel stage: where the product sits on the path from free to paid. "Lead Magnet" is a free item that collects emails, "Tripwire" is a small first purchase, "Core" is the main offer, "Upsell" is an add-on, and "Continuity" is a repeat or subscription offer.
- Listing: the product's page on one outlet.
- Outlet: the place it is listed, such as Amazon KDP, Kindle, Etsy, Payhip, Gumroad or your own store.
- Status: how far along the product is, from "Idea" through "Drafting", "Needs Cover", "Proof Ordered" and "Ready To Upload" to "Live" and "Twin Listed". "Cut" means it was dropped.
- "Pipeline": the production job for the product.

## Good to know

- The product type is guessed from words in the name. "word search" or "puzzle" gives a puzzle book; "planner", "tracker", "log" or "journal" gives a journal or tracker; "coloring" a coloring book; "activity" a kids activity book; "bundle" a bundle; "template" a Canva template; "guide", "ebook" or "book" a written book; anything else a printable. Change it in the form if the guess is wrong.
- Outlets map to destinations this way: Amazon KDP and Kindle go to KDP, Etsy, Payhip and Gumroad go to their own playbooks, and "Own store" goes to your own site.
- Products are added and edited on the Products page (titled "Products & Courses"), not here. A product shows on this tab once it is linked to the brand. On that page the link is made in the product's "Channels promoting this product" list, which only offers projects of the content channel type. Not confirmed: how products get linked to brands of other types.
- "Start production" does not list or publish anything. Listing is a step in the pipeline that you do yourself.
- If a product has more than one pipeline, "Pipeline" opens the newest.
