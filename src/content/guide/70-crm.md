# CRM
<!-- part: Clients and leads | order: 70 | slug: crm | route: /crm | tags: crm, pipeline, deals, stages, companies, contacts, follow-ups, interactions, inbound leads, promote, create project, won deal, lead analysis, outreach drafts -->

The CRM (customer relationship manager) is where you track the businesses you are selling to: the companies, the people at them, the deals you are working and the follow-ups you owe. Its "Inbound" tab is also where new leads from the website and the lead qualifiers land first. Use it every day to answer new leads, move deals forward and turn a won deal into a client project.

## Where to find it

- On a computer, open the "Clients & Leads" group in the side menu and choose "CRM".
- On a phone, tap "More" in the bottom bar, then "CRM" under "Clients & Leads".
- The page opens on the "Pipeline" tab.

## What you see

- Five tabs: "Pipeline", "Companies", "Contacts", "Follow-ups" (with a count when something is due) and "Inbound" (with a count of new leads).
- "Pipeline": deals in five columns: Lead, Pitch, Proposal, Won and Lost. Each card has a stage selector that can move the deal to any stage.
- "Companies": a "New company name…" box, a "Show archived" switch and a card per company with its status ("prospect", "active" or "past") and "linked" when it has a project.
- "Contacts": a read-only list of everyone you have added.
- "Follow-ups": "Follow-ups Due", the logged interactions whose follow-up date is today or earlier. Overdue dates are red.
- "Inbound": average first-response times, the sub-tabs "New", "Reviewed", "Promoted" and "Archived", and a card per lead with the name, business, industry, urgency, "Ready" badge, email, time and message.
- A company's panel (tap any company): suggestions, "View Project" or "Create Project from this Company", "Archive or delete…", "Contacts", "Deals", "Log Interaction", "Timeline", "Lead Analysis", "Outreach Drafts" and "Proposals".

## How to use it

### Answer a new lead
1. Open "Inbound", then "New". Unanswered emergency leads sit at the top with a red edge and a running timer.
2. If the lead has no urgency, choose one under "Set urgency:".
3. After you contact the lead, tap "Mark responded".
4. Then tap "Promote to CRM", "Create project from lead", "Mark reviewed" or "Archive".

### Promote a lead into the pipeline
1. Tap "Promote to CRM" on a New or Reviewed lead. HQ creates the company, primary contact (including phone) and a Lead-stage deal together. Repeating the request returns the same company.
2. Open the company to edit its details or the new deal. Add another deal only when it is a separate opportunity.

### Add a company or contact yourself
1. On "Companies", type a name in "New company name…" and tap "Add".
2. Tap the company, fill in "First", "Last" and "Email" under "Contacts", and tap the plus button.

### Move a deal and log activity
1. On "Pipeline", choose a stage on the card. Moving to Won or Lost records that outcome date; reopening clears the old outcome dates.
2. To log a call, email, meeting or note, open the company, pick the type under "Log Interaction", add an optional follow-up date, write a summary and tap "Log". It appears in "Timeline", and a follow-up date puts it on "Follow-ups" from that day.

### Turn a lead or a won deal into a client project
1. From a lead, tap "Create project from lead". An "Add New Project" window opens with a "From inbound lead" box marked "Pre-filled · confirm or edit". Check the name and project type (choose the appropriate client industry type; client types share the client layout), then tap "Add Project". The lead becomes "Promoted" and the setup wizard for the new client opens.
2. From a deal in "Won", open the company and tap "Create Project from this Company". HQ creates a client project with the company's name, marks the company "active" and "linked", and opens the Dashboard.

### Research a company and draft outreach
1. Under "Lead Analysis", tap "Analyze Lead", choose "Website URL" or "Paste details", then "Run analysis". You get a priority, a matched offer, a summary and "Why this offer".
2. Under "Outreach Drafts", tap "Generate Outreach", pick a "Channel", "Tone" and "Follow-up days", then "Draft outreach". Edit, copy and "Save changes". Nothing is sent from here.

### Archive or delete a company
1. Use the three-dot menu on its card, or "Archive or delete…" in its panel. The window lists what deleting would destroy or unlink.
2. Choose "Archive (recommended)" or "Delete permanently". "Show archived" lets you find and restore archived companies.


### Find and sort CRM records
1. In "Pipeline", search by deal title or company. Choose newest, oldest, highest deal value or title order.
2. Stage headings use both names and colors. On smaller screens, stages stack vertically; on a computer, scroll across the board when needed.
3. In "Companies", search by company or industry and sort by name, newest or oldest. "Add company" opens this tab and focuses the name box.
4. In "Contacts", search by name, email or phone. Matching contacts appear alphabetically.

## Words on this screen

- Lead, Pitch, Proposal, Won, Lost: the five deal stages, in order.
- Promoted: the lead has become a CRM company or a project.
- Ready: the qualifier judged the lead qualified for its industry.
- Urgency: "EMERGENCY", "Same day", "Routine", "Estimate" or "Maintenance".
- Avg first response: average time from when urgency was set to the first response, counted only for leads with an urgency.
- linked: the company has a client project.
- Archive: hides a record but keeps it and everything tied to it.

## Good to know

- The CRM covers your whole account. The project picker in the header does not filter it.
- A lead can show "Responded" because the automatic thank-you email went out, not because you replied.
- Inbound cards show the phone number, available qualifier answers and conversation transcript. Expand the details to review them.
- Reviewed leads can still be promoted to the CRM.
- Deals can move backward, forward or directly to Lost with the stage selector.
- Tap the completion control on a due follow-up to remove it from the due list while retaining its timeline entry.
- Open the company panel and use Edit company, Edit contact or Edit deal to correct saved details. Archived-deal browsing remains a separate limitation.
- A project made with "Create Project from this Company" has no capture link until its settings are saved once ("Admin Panel", then "Settings", then "Projects"), and a new project may not show in the header picker until you reload the page.
