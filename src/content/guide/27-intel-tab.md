# Intel tab
<!-- part: Running a brand | order: 27 | slug: intel-tab | route: /project/:venueId | tags: intel, research, niche profile, pains, top pains, briefs, ideas, idea score, make this, pipeline, dimension library, answer bank, radar -->

The "Intel" tab holds this brand's research: its niche profile, the audience's top pains, the scored idea briefs waiting to be made, and the shared library of content patterns. It is where research turns into work, through the "Make this" buttons. Use it to see what this brand's audience wants and to pick the next piece to make.

## Where to find it

- Open the brand, then tap "Intel" in the tab row.
- Or, on a pipeline page, tap the niche profile's name next to "Niche profile:".
- The address ends in ?tab=intel.

## What you see

- "Niche Profile": the research write-up on this brand's niche, with its title and the start of its summary. Long profiles end with "Show full profile". With none, it says "No niche profile yet."
- "Top pains": up to eight of the audience's biggest problems, highest score first. Each shows its rank number, the pain in the audience's words, "Score" with its pain score, how many sources mention it, the word "Product" when it has been flagged as a product idea, and a "Make this" button. With none, it says "No pains recorded yet."
- "Briefs": idea briefs for this brand that are still at the idea stage and have an idea score, highest first. Each shows the score in large type, the title, the format and purpose, and a "Make this" button, or "Pipeline" once a pipeline exists. With none, it says "No scored ideas yet."
- "Dimension library": small tags for the content patterns filed in research. Each tag starts with its shelf in bold, such as "Structure", followed by the pattern's name. With none, it says "No dimensions filed yet."

## How to use it

### To make a brief
1. Under "Briefs", tap "Make this".
2. The "Create pipeline" form opens with the brief's title, a product type based on its format, and the usual content choices: lead magnet and affiliate for monetization, and post to lead magnet for promotion, when those playbooks exist.
3. Check the choices and tap "Create pipeline". The new pipeline page opens, and the brief's button becomes "Pipeline".

### To turn a pain into a piece
1. Under "Top pains", tap "Make this".
2. HQ first creates a new idea titled "Answer:" followed by the pain, then opens the "Create pipeline" form for it as a social piece.
3. Check the choices and tap "Create pipeline". The new pipeline page opens.

### To read the full niche profile
1. Tap "Show full profile" under the summary.
2. Tap "Show less" to fold it again.

### To open a brief's pipeline
1. Tap "Pipeline" on the brief.

## Words on this screen

- Niche profile: the research summary of who the brand's audience is and what they want.
- Pain: a problem the audience keeps raising, gathered in research. The number of sources shows how often it came up.
- Pain score: how strong the pain is. The Answer Bank page shows the same score out of 25.
- "Product": the pain has been flagged as a good idea for a product.
- Brief: a scored content idea that has not been made yet.
- Idea score: how the idea scored in research. Higher is better.
- Format and purpose: the shape of the piece (such as short or article) and what it is for (such as teach or offer).
- Dimension library: patterns that research found in winning content, sorted onto shelves.
- "Make this": start a pipeline for the brief or pain.
- "Pipeline": open the pipeline already made for the brief.

## Good to know

- "Make this" on a pain creates the new idea the moment you tap it, before the form opens. If you then cancel the form, the idea stays on the brand's list. The pain row never changes to "Pipeline", so tapping "Make this" again on the same pain creates another idea.
- An idea made from a pain gets the purpose teach and a note that it came from the Answer Bank on that day.
- The same pains, with more detail and the same "Make this" button, are on the Answer Bank page. The same briefs are on the Radar page, where "Make this" disappears once a pipeline has been made.
- The dimension library comes from shared research, so every brand shows the same list.
- This tab only reads research. The monthly Radar run, on the 1st of each month, adds the new briefs, refreshes niche profiles and files complaints in the Answer Bank. The nightly production run can also add pains when one of its steps mines complaints. The "Activity" list on the "Overview" tab shows when research runs happened.
