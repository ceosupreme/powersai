# Affiliate Programs
<!-- part: Money and offers | order: 64 | slug: affiliate-programs | route: /affiliate-programs | tags: affiliate, affiliate programs, commissions, partner programs, referral links, niche, applied, approved, active, rejected, paused -->

Affiliate Programs is one shared list of the affiliate programs you have joined or applied to, with each program's niche, commission, link and status. Use it as your reference for a program's link and terms, and to track where each application stands.

## Where to find it
- On a computer, tap "Affiliate Programs" under "Brand & Assets" in the left menu.
- On a phone, tap "More" in the bottom bar, then "Affiliate Programs" under "Brand & Assets".
- It is one list for your whole account. The project picker at the top of the screen does not change it.

## What you see
- The heading "Affiliate Programs", the line "Shared library across all channels", and a "New Program" button.
- A tip box titled "Account-wide library". You can close it with its X.
- A table with "Name", "Niche", "Commission", "Status", "Link" (a "Visit" link that opens in a new tab), and edit and delete buttons. On a phone, swipe it sideways.
- With no programs yet, the table says "No affiliate programs yet."

## How to use it
### Add a program
1. Tap "New Program".
2. Type the "Name". It is the only required field.
3. Fill in "Niche" and pick a "Status". New programs start as applied.
4. Pick a "Commission Type" and type the "Commission Detail", for example 8% or $10/sale.
5. Paste the program's "Link" and add any "Notes".
6. Tap "Add". A "Program added" message confirms it.

### Update a program as it moves along
1. Tap the pencil on the program's row.
2. Change the "Status", for example from applied to approved, then to active.
3. Tap "Save".

### Delete a program
1. Tap the trash button on the program's row.
2. Confirm "Delete this program?".

## Words on this screen
- Affiliate program: a company's program that pays you a commission when people buy through your link.
- Niche: the topic or audience the program fits.
- Commission Type: percentage (a share of each sale), flat (a fixed amount per sale) or hybrid (a mix of both).
- Commission Detail: the actual terms in your own words, shown after the type in the "Commission" column.
- Status: applied, approved, active, rejected or paused.
- Link: your affiliate or sign-up web address for the program.

## Good to know
- The tip box says you can reference programs from any project's content, campaigns or revenue entries, but no other HQ screen reads this list in this build. A content item's "Affiliate Link" is a separate box you type into, and affiliate income is logged on Channel Revenue with the type "Affiliate".
- "Notes" are saved but not shown in the table; open the program to read them.
