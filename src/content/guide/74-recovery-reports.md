# Recovery Reports
<!-- part: Clients and leads | order: 74 | slug: recovery-reports | route: /automations/recovery-reports | tags: recovery report, weekly report, client report, estimated value, narrative, share link, public link, print, pdf, referral footer -->

A Recovery Report is a weekly one-page summary for a client: leads captured, follow-ups that re-engaged, customers reactivated and new reviews, with an estimated dollar value. HQ drafts one per client each week for you to check, edit and share. A client only sees a report when you share it.

## Where to find it

- On a computer, open "Clients & Leads" in the side menu and choose "Recovery Reports". On a phone, tap "More", then "Recovery Reports".
- A client's project page also has a "Recovery Reports" shortcut card.
- The list follows the project chosen in the header.
- A report you share opens for the client at a public link that starts with /r/.

## What you see

- A list of weeks, each with its date range, a status ("draft", "reviewed" or "sent") and the estimate.
- For the selected week, an "Est. … at work" card with the average ticket and close rate it used, and "How we got this" with the formula and caveats.
- Four numbers: "Leads captured", "Follow-ups re-engaged", "Reactivation responded" and "Reviews landed".
- A "Narrative (editable)" box and the buttons "View branded report", "Copy for client", and "Mark reviewed" or "Mark sent".
- The branded view shows the client-facing design, titled "Weekly Recovery Report" and "Prepared for" the client. Its top bar has "Back", a "Show referral footer" switch, the status, "Create share link" (or "Copy link" and "Revoke") and "Print / PDF". Drafts carry a "DRAFT" watermark.

## How to use it

### Review a weekly draft
1. Choose the client in the header and tap the week.
2. Check the numbers and open "How we got this".
3. Edit the narrative. It saves when you tap outside the box.
4. Tap "Mark reviewed".

### Share it with the client
1. Tap "View branded report".
2. Turn on "Show referral footer" if you want the referral note at the bottom.
3. Tap "Create share link" (possible once the report is reviewed or sent), then "Copy link", and send it. Or tap "Print / PDF" to save a PDF.
4. Tap "Back", then "Mark sent".
5. As another option, "Copy for client" copies a text summary you can paste into an email.

### Stop a shared link
1. Open the branded view and tap "Revoke", then confirm.
2. Anyone who opens the old link now sees "Report unavailable". Creating a new link gives a new address.

## Words on this screen

- draft, reviewed, sent: the three report statuses. Only reviewed or sent reports can be shared.
- Est. … at work: the estimated value of the week's recovered activity.
- avg ticket, close rate: the average sale and the share of leads that buy, both used in the estimate.
- source: "project", "default" or "mixed" tells you whether the estimate used the client's own figures or built-in defaults.
- Follow-ups re-engaged: leads marked ready after a follow-up went out. It is not a confirmed reply.
- Reactivation responded: win-back messages delivered. It is not a confirmed reply either.
- Reviews landed: new Google reviews that week.
- Referral footer: an optional closing note that invites the client to refer other owners.

## Good to know

- Drafts are made every Monday morning (8 AM Pacific in winter, 9 AM in summer) for the previous Monday to Sunday, and only for active projects with at least one automation turned on. "No reports yet" usually means no client meets that yet.
- Once you edit the narrative or mark a report reviewed or sent, the weekly job leaves it alone.
- There is no field for a client's own average ticket or close rate, so estimates use built-in defaults, and the caveats say so.
- With "All projects" selected the list mixes clients without showing their names, and the branded view says "your business". Pick a client first.
- "Copy for client" includes formatting symbols such as # and asterisks, which show as plain characters in some apps.
- The public report page needs no login, is hidden from search engines and shows only the client-facing report.

Set Average sale and Close rate in the project’s Edit form to use project-specific economics. Recovery calculations prefer these values, then existing period values and defaults when unset. Estimates remain estimates, not confirmed revenue.
