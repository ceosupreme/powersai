# Supreme Team Media — Growth Program Offer Framework (v1)
**Internal sales reference, not a public price commitment.** Validate pricing and exact deliverables against each signed proposal. The same system architecture can be sold at different rates when scope, deployment intensity, market count, integrations and media production differ; do not suggest that unlike offers are interchangeable.

## Core promise
**We build and run the system that gets the right people interested, turns interest into real conversations and brings good customers back.** Strategy, creative, follow-up and performance improvement work together—not just a website, ad campaign or software login.

## The five connected outcomes
1. **Be found:** search-ready pages, local presence, helpful content and selected campaigns where justified.
2. **Capture interest:** a useful offer and simple inquiry/booking paths that record source and consent.
3. **Respond and follow through:** timely handoff, reminders and client-specific automation where configured.
4. **Convert:** clear qualification, appointments, proposal/checkout and a named person responsible for closing.
5. **Retain and improve:** reviews, reactivation, customer communications and a scorecard tied to business outcomes.

## What the client actually buys
A managed growth operation with (a) a defined initial foundation/build, (b) ongoing creative, marketing and automation management as contracted, and (c) a transparent monthly review. HQ is our operational control system; HighLevel may be the underlying CRM and follow-up platform. Neither is the headline product.

## Pricing architecture — keep components distinct
- **Foundation / setup fee:** price the actual discovery, integrations, brand/media preparation and launch deliverables, with payment milestones tied to acceptance conditions.
- **Monthly management fee:** price the cadence, platforms, channels, number of active markets, content output and reporting/support commitments. It starts at the event defined in that client's agreement.
- **Paid media and third-party costs:** separately identified as client-funded wherever applicable.
- **Pilot (optional):** a specific short test with narrow deliverables; state explicitly whether and when the pilot is credited.
- **Expansion:** new markets, advanced integrations, extra locations and custom development priced and approved before committing.

Do not collapse CPN, Keegan and Finca into one public rate without reconciling the very different delivery models. A `$2,500 website` is a standalone build/downsell, **not** a substitute for the managed Growth Program.

## Standard sales sequence
1. Discovery: buyer's goal, economics, operating constraints, and existing tools
2. Short diagnosis: where opportunities are lost, with evidence
3. Proposal: specific outcome, first 30 days, responsibility split, total investment and next step
4. Agreement: use one standard STM agreement structure with the *selected program, fees, milestones, service levels and dependencies* as scoped exhibits
5. Client start kit: trigger only on signed agreement and payment/start authorization
6. Monthly review: client sees the signals we can actually verify

## Qualification
Good fit: an operating business with genuine demand and a decision maker who can approve creative, account access and follow-up. Poor fit: a buyer who expects guaranteed sales without staff follow-through, or who cannot provide the access/consent essential to their program.
