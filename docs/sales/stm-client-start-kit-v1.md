# Supreme Team Media — Client Start Kit (v1)
Internal reusable working template. **Do not send automatically.** Fill the project-specific fields and confirm payment/approval before sending. This kit does not alter the contract, establish new guarantees, or require a client to share passwords.

## When to start
Trigger: a signed agreement AND the agreed initial payment or signed-off start authorization, as applicable to that engagement. Record the actual date, amount and receipt reference. Never infer payment from a proposal request.

## 1. Welcome email — send only after the trigger

**Subject:** Welcome to Supreme Team Media — here's what happens next

Hi [First name],

We're officially getting started on [Program or project]. We're excited to build the systems that help [Company] attract more of the right people, respond faster, and turn more opportunities into business.

Here's the plan:
1. **Your kickoff:** [Date/time and location or scheduling link]. We'll confirm the first priorities and who approves what.
2. **Your short intake:** [Intake form URL]. It gives us the details to start without chasing scattered emails.
3. **Your access and assets:** We'll send secure, specific invitations for the accounts we need. Please don't email passwords. We can use delegated permissions and shared folders.
4. **Your first delivery:** [Contracted first milestone and date or delivery window]. You'll know what needs your approval and what we're handling.

Your main point of contact is [Person / email]. If an urgent change comes up, reply here and we'll coordinate it.

We’re looking forward to getting real work into motion.

Supreme Team Media

## 2. Intake form — fields to create in GoHighLevel/HQ
Keep this in one mobile-friendly form, and prefill known details. Mark only essential fields required.

**Business basics**
- Legal business name, public-facing name, website, main city/market, additional locations
- Primary contact name, role, email, phone and preferred communication channel
- Billing contact, if different
- Industry, products/services, geographic service areas
- Business hours and any seasonal/event schedule

**Outcomes and priorities**
- The single business outcome they most want improved in the next 90 days
- Their top three goals (lead generation, appointments, repeat visits, average purchase, reviews, launch, etc.)
- Who the ideal buyer/customer is; who should not be targeted
- Current process: how a new inquiry arrives, who responds, how it is booked/closed
- Known bottleneck (slow response, poor quality leads, no-shows, unclear offer, retention)
- Existing metrics and sources; **unknown is acceptable**, never force estimates to become facts

**Offer and operations**
- Current products/services, price ranges and active promotions
- Terms or claims we must honor or avoid; compliance/regulated-industry limitations
- Locations, service capacity, blackout days and important upcoming events
- What qualifies as a good inquiry or booked appointment
- Named approver and normal response time for approvals

**Access and materials**
- Logo/brand kit, photos, videos, testimonials and example content URLs
- Links to website/domain, analytics, Google Business Profile, social pages, booking platform, CRM and payments
- Choose owner or delegated access for each platform; provide invitations only, **never passwords or recovery codes**
- Permission to use existing approved imagery, brand assets and customer reviews
- Published privacy policy and consent language, if campaigns or SMS will be used

**Approval**
- Confirm main client contact and internal STM owner
- Checkbox acknowledging that advertising budgets, integrations and third-party subscriptions require separate account authorization where applicable
- Submitter name and timestamp

## 3. Day-one internal delivery checklist
- [ ] Confirm signed engagement, agreed scope, payment/start conditions and client-owned accounts
- [ ] Create or update client in **STM Clients** pipeline; link original **STM Sales** opportunity
- [ ] Save primary contact, stakeholders and preferred contact method
- [ ] Send the welcome email (only after signed/start trigger)
- [ ] Send intake link; make missing essentials visible
- [ ] Schedule kickoff, assign accountable STM lead and name client approver
- [ ] Create a client-specific HQ project/work plan with due dates for first milestone
- [ ] Request delegated account access; do not request plaintext passwords
- [ ] Collect current baseline: inquiries, lead-response time, bookings, conversions, reviews and revenue **only when actually available**
- [ ] Agree on what a qualified lead, an appointment and success mean for this project
- [ ] Confirm brand voice, approval workflow, copy/claims restrictions and launch criteria
- [ ] Build first deliverable(s) and enter the next review date
- [ ] Set monthly review cadence and reporting scope based on contract
- [ ] Mark missing items explicitly as Waiting on client / Waiting on STM

## 5. Implementation notes
- The intake can be built as a GoHighLevel form; default submissions should create/update the correct contact and open a scoped client-onboarding task, not blast a generic campaign.
- Do **not** use this kit to replace a signed scope of work. Proposal prices and milestones differ by client; use the actual accepted engagement.
- Do not trigger SMS or review requests before consent and relevant automation configuration are verified.
